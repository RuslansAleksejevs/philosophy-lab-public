# Administrative closure of the twenty independent verification reports

This additive note leaves the frozen scientific METHOD and every historical
attempt unchanged. It adds a typed, multi-session evidence binding for the
already authorized coordinator decision. It launches no reviewer, revision,
provider operation, publication, or submission.

Typed multi-session verification bindings preserve each original author and session. Critiques concern the frozen manuscripts, not the later coordinator synthesis. The legacy single-author checkpoint is administrative INCOMPLETE; the separate finite receipt records only the explicitly accepted verification and recommendation. No novelty certificate, manuscript authorship transfer, whole-provider coverage, or submission approval is implied.

The standard question is retained. The four later typed stages bind:

- **Sources:** ten accepted literature reports, their exact source notes, access
  limits and web evidence. The source ledger is the coordinator's compilation;
  the individual retrieval and audit work keeps its real worker session.
- **Contribution:** the coordinator's ten-row comparison of what survives each
  argument/source audit, with the exact original manuscript hashes. This is
  a verification synthesis, not new authorship of the historical manuscripts.
- **Critique:** a mechanical index of all twenty accepted independent reports,
  with per-item author/session/file and capture references. It does not pretend
  those workers saw or reviewed the later comparison or recommendation.
- **Decision:** the coordinator's explanation for all ten manuscripts and, when
  support permits, three recommendations plus two reserves and five short cards.
  A declared unresolved blocking defect prevents selecting that manuscript.

The standard checkpoint API is called with `incomplete=True` and outcome
`CONTINUE`, because its single-author stage schema cannot represent this
multi-session binding honestly. Its historical administrative state remains
visible. The distinct `VERIFICATION_COMPLETE_BOUNDED` receipt requires all
20 canonical accepted reports, unchanged originals, the full comparison, and
an actual 3+2 recommendation. If the evidence does not support five choices,
use `WITHHELD_FOR_DEFECTS`: the helper preserves an incomplete recommendation
receipt and never manufactures a successful selection.

The helper checks identities, bytes, coverage links and declared decision
constraints. It cannot certify the truth or quality of the coordinator's
judgment. Root must check the cited passages after canonical acceptance and
supply its own captured AI explanation. Human instructions authorize the
work; they never serve as evidence that the AI authored that explanation.

The already accepted V01-A and V02-A results retain their V3 transport parent.
The other eighteen results must bind the accepted V4 successor; its queue must
equal the preserved V3 queue. Partial CLI stdout remains explicitly partial.
Acceptance under V4 relies on its exact bounded native replay, not on a claim
that every CLI rendering or every provider record was retained.
