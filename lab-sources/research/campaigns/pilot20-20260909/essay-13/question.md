# Question
Can a future branching event change whether an ordinary object has already persisted through an earlier interval?

Initial thesis to investigate: a criterion of completed persistence should satisfy prefix invariance: two possible histories that agree throughout a causally sufficient history up to t should not differ about whether a designated object at s has survived to t merely because they diverge after t. This is not a denial that future history individuates maximal temporal worms. It is a proposed distinction between completed persistence and maximal career membership. The contribution will test whether familiar nonbranching conditions illicitly transfer the latter's globality to the former, and examine whether genuine anticipation or teleology defeats the constraint.
