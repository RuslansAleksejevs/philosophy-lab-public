Develop a narrower argument: additionality is a causal criterion, whereas permission requires a justified specification of the available alternatives. An accurately forecast, genuinely additional restoration project can fail to justify destruction when its conditionality is created by an actor who lacks justification for withholding restoration. Distinguish this from scarce resources where development actually finances otherwise unavailable restoration. The contribution is a proposed reason-sensitive test of conditionality, including a distinction between what an authority should accept under pressure and what a proponent is entitled to offer. This is a tentative synthesis and extension, not established novelty.
