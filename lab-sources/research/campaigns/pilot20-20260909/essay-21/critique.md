# Critique of essay 21

Verdict: REVISE. The central conditional argument is valid and clearly explained, but the draft currently establishes a modest comparison principle while implying a larger intervention in the adaptive-desire debate. The main revision should strengthen its dialectical target and define the objects of comparison, rather than expand its formal apparatus.

## Strongest reconstruction

The essay permits newly acquired satisfactions to have their full welfare value. It distinguishes remedial improvement after a loss, restoration relative to an ordinary no-intervention history, and prospective ranking against all relevant feasible interventions. If enrichment can be supplied with equal later welfare without an earlier loss, and no whole-life advantage reverses that earlier disadvantage, the loss-plus-enrichment option is worse. Accordingly, recognizing adaptation as beneficial does not require approving the route that occasioned it.

This succeeds as an existence and compatibility result. It does not show that adaptation generally fails to compensate, that desire theories ordinarily approve unnecessary losses, or that welfare suboptimality entails impermissibility. The manuscript mostly respects these limits. Its strongest feature is allowing adaptation to compensate relative to one baseline while retaining a reason to choose another route.

## 1. Identify a real disputed inference

The opening says discussions easily move between benefit, compensation and justification, and describes an apparent choice between distrusting new desires and vindicating deprivation. Yet the sources actually cited establish disputes about whether creating satisfied desires is beneficial, not a general commitment to ignoring superior alternatives. A proactive satisfactionist can accept every ranking in the choir case immediately. She can also favor changing desires in cases where doing so produces the best attainable life. Your argument neither refutes her nor resolves the familiar objection to a welfare theory that approves engineered preferences when engineering is the best option.

The most serious counterexample to the essay's ambition is painless instantaneous adaptation. Suppose a controller closes the choir and changes Mira's interests before any frustration or missed intrinsically desired experience occurs; the two histories have equal lifetime satisfaction. Many critics of adaptive preferences may still object, but your first condition fails. Similarly, when loss is indispensable to the best feasible satisfaction, your framework may approve it. This is not a counterexample to the conditional theorem. It shows the theorem leaves central concerns about adaptation unresolved.

Revision: explicitly frame the paper as a limited answer to the inference from recovery's value to the harmful route's superiority. Provide a documented example of that inference if possible; otherwise call it a tempting inference rather than a literature-wide dilemma. Add a short limiting case in which your account gives no prudential objection, and explain why that is an accepted cost. Retain the compatibility result; do not market it as a general rescue of subjectivism from adaptive-preference objections.

## 2. Specify which event the compensation counterfactual removes

The draft alternates between absence of closure, absence of an action, and absence of the whole intervention. These are not automatically the same counterfactual. If the organization had already arranged ceramics, removing the closure leaves enrichment in place. If ceramics is a discretionary recovery response triggered by closure, removing closure may also remove ceramics. If the evaluated event is the entire closure-and-enrichment package, its absence is a third question.

Consider two agencies producing exactly the same harmful closure and pottery benefit. Agency A had independently promised the class; agency B offers it only after closure. On a literal ordinary-counterfactual test, A may fail to compensate while B compensates, even though the victim receives the same history. That can be coherent as a causal comparison, but it is awkward if presented as an unqualified finding about compensation. A prior duty to supply the class makes the word more contentious still. The manuscript excludes legal entitlements, but that does not eliminate the conceptual ambiguity.

Revision: stipulate the act or event removed in each comparison and distinguish descriptive welfare restoration from compensatory credit. Say explicitly that restoring welfare relative to a selected baseline is not sufficient to settle whether a benefit counts as discharging a compensatory obligation. Avoid “actual no-loss history”: the history is counterfactual, and its reference event needs naming. The numerical example should state that 10 is the no-package history, not silently every no-closure history.

## 3. Opportunity cost is a close established debate, not merely a generic objection

An especially close primary precedent is Carlson, Johansson and Risberg, “Prudential Problems for the Counterfactual Comparative Account of Harm and Benefit,” opening, Three/Four Buttons and Claims 1–4, https://doi.org/10.1093/pq/pqad082. Their argument explicitly separates counterfactual benefit from choice rankings and treats the divergence as an objection. Your essay instead accommodates that divergence. Engage this disagreement: explain why the predicate “benefits relative to what otherwise happens” need not determine prudential choice. This could make the contribution more philosophically substantive than renaming opportunity cost. I checked those opening arguments, not the complete later discussion.

Eggert's “Compensating beneficiaries,” abstract and §1, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11098-024-02150-6, also makes compensation of net beneficiaries and counterfactual selection central. Her rectificatory project differs from yours, but requires acknowledgment if you retain the broad compensation framing. Do not claim that your distinction settles her question.

The genuine contribution presently available is a focused application: adapting desires can count fully while the causal contribution attributed to deprivation is corrected by a feasible alternative. Novelty of that application remains unestablished. The new name alone adds no originality.

## 4. Separate welfare ranking from prudential requirement

Temporal dominance yields a better life, given the conditions. It does not alone show that choosing a less good life is prudentially impermissible. A satisficing or prerogative-based account could allow Mira to choose 12 over 15. The organization may have role obligations absent from Mira's personal prudence. The essay's occasional “requires” and “justified” language outruns its uncontroversial ranking argument, despite later qualifications.

Revision: consistently conclude that recovery provides insufficient evidence that the package is best for her, or that there is a welfare reason favoring the alternative. If you want a requirement, defend the further decision principle and specify its agent. Also distinguish evaluating actual outcomes from decisions under uncertainty: equal realized later welfare is not equal expected welfare. A brief deterministic-scope stipulation is sufficient here.

## 5. Portability and the third condition

The portable-recovery constraint is secure partly because the third condition excludes any holistic feature that could reverse its conclusion. That is acceptable for a conditional dominance principle, but it transfers the substantive burden to checking the conditions. The current “evidence needed” paragraph is the right direction. Strengthen it by stating what supports transfer in the example: a prearranged invitation, known willingness to attend, compatible scheduling, and no loss-dependent benefit. Do not infer transfer merely because identical goods can be described in both histories.

A stronger version of your own case could admit different activities and different desires in the two histories, provided independently supported welfare rankings remain equal. Exact psychological duplication is more than dominance needs. Keep clear that sameness of desire strength or number alone does not establish equality for every desire theory.

## Source accuracy and scholarly scope

Dietz (2023), §§2–3 and §4.2, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11098-023-01924-8, supports the proactive/reactive background and treatment of wrong-origin exclusions. Your summary is accurate. His distinction does not itself endorse the false dichotomy in your opening.

Heathwood (2006), §1.1, printed pp.541–542, https://spot.colorado.edu/~heathwoo/DSH.pdf, accurately supports your changing-desire discussion. Keep your present limited phrasing: concurrence is an intermediate proposal with acknowledged unresolved temporal questions.

Bruckner (2013), institutional abstract, https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/present-desire-satisfaction-and-past-well-being/, supports your stated warning about later fulfilment and past welfare. I also accessed only that abstract; the DOI retrieval failed. Your explicit access limitation is responsible. A completed event's occurrence being fixed does not by itself fix its welfare value, but your holistic qualification recognizes that distinction.

## Readability and revision order

The opening makes the stakes intelligible; the choir example earns its place. There is no serious jargon barrier. The repeated lists of what the argument does not show, however, dilute the positive result. Consolidate the limits once, retain the portability objection, and use the saved space for the event-individuation problem and the close prudence precedent. The numerical paragraph says its numbers are merely rankings but then subtracts units: ordinal rankings license ordering, not meaningful difference sizes. Either stipulate an illustrative cardinal welfare scale or report only the three orderings.

Priority order: (1) delimit the target and acknowledge the close precedent; (2) fix the compensation/reference-event ambiguity; (3) separate welfare superiority from prudential obligation; (4) add a clear scope counterexample and repair the numerical wording. Preserve the draft's central insight and its willingness to count adaptive fulfilment; neither needs abandoning.
