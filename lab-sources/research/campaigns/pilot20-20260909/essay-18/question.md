# Question: understanding with unresolved models

When incompatible causal models fit the available evidence, what can a scientist understand about why an outcome occurred?

Tentative AI-originated thesis: disagreement among live models can warrant a precisely limited explanatory understanding when the answer to a contrastive why-question is invariant across the models, but merely agreeing on the actual outcome is insufficient. Understanding additionally requires tracking which counterfactual claims survive model disagreement and where the explanatory answer changes. The contribution to investigate is an argument for separating an invariant explanatory core from conditional explanatory branches, and for why correctly locating the unresolved boundary can constitute an epistemic achievement rather than mere confessed ignorance.

Research will examine counterfactual accounts of understanding, robustness analysis, and claims about understanding through false models. The proposal is exploratory, not a novelty claim. Cases and the final thesis remain to be developed by this author.
