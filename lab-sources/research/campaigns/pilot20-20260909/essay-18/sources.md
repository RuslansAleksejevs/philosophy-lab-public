## Revision of exploratory thesis
The initial stable explanatory-core proposal overlaps directly with existing robustness and stable-understanding accounts. It is not the claimed contribution. Develop instead a limitation on pointwise accounts of partial understanding: two model families can agree on all individual intervention-answer sets yet differ on relations among unresolved counterfactual answers. Such relations support different warranted transfers of experimental learning.

The planned original toy example compares Y=X versus Y=Z with Y=X AND Z versus Y=X OR Z. With actual X=Z=1, each family has identical answer sets for every binary input configuration. But the two mixed-input responses must differ in the first family and coincide in the second. The explanatory achievement is grasping the causal organization of remaining uncertainty. It is compatible with a sufficiently expressive stability account and with established joint identification; the proposed novelty is philosophical diagnosis and argument, not a novel mathematical construction claim.

## Strong objections to address
A relational claim is itself a robust answer if the question language is enlarged; concede this and reject only pointwise reduction. A model set can omit truth; justify coverage separately. Mere confidence management is not target understanding; distinguish causal structural exclusions from facts about personal ignorance. Understanding need not require exhaustive metacognition; scope the requirement to claims of understanding across the disputed contrast family.
