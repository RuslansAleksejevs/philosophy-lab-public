# Critique: Understanding What Remains Unresolved

## Verdict and reconstruction

Revise and retain the central example. The essay proves a precise representational loss and offers a plausible case of partial causal understanding. Its main unresolved issue is whether the example contributes an account of explanatory understanding, beyond an instructive demonstration that projections discard information. The draft is admirably restrained about mathematics and novelty, but its philosophical opponent and the sufficiency of its positive criterion require sharpening.

The argument is: (1) Selection and Combination have identical sets of possible outcomes at each setting; (2) their jointly possible response profiles differ; (3) knowing the appropriate family therefore warrants different transfers from one experimental result to another; (4) these differences reflect constraints on the actual device's causal organization; so (5) preserving separate intervention answers need not preserve partial causal understanding. Steps1–3 are correct. Step4 is plausible under the stipulated construction evidence and stable-device assumptions. Step5 is defensible as a necessary preservation constraint, though not yet a sufficient characterization of understanding.

The genuine candidate contribution is the use of this pair to expose what a question-by-question assessment misses, coupled with an argument that some jointly constrained ignorance is positive causal understanding. Stable understanding, relational information, joint identification and the broad importance of inferential ability are background. The essay correctly acknowledges much of this.

## Priority 1: distinguish the preservation result from an understanding criterion

The text sometimes moves from a solid necessary condition to a much stronger sufficiency claim: any genuine nontrivial constraint on explanations that supports discriminating inference seems enough for partial explanatory understanding. That criterion is too permissive without a clearer demand on grasp.

Counterexample: an investigator receives a certified lookup card saying A and B differ. They can mechanically infer B from A, but cannot identify which physical changes A and B denote, explain why stability across tests matters, or distinguish changing the model hypothesis from manipulating the device. They have reliable conditional information and pass a bare transfer test, yet the attribution of causal understanding is doubtful. A second version knows the intervention labels but learned the relation by memorizing a trusted report, with no recognition of the asymmetry it encodes. Whether this already constitutes minimal understanding is exactly what the essay must decide, not settle through the word “grasp.”

Repair: explicitly present the result as a constraint on faithful representation/assessment of already warranted causal understanding. If offering positive sufficient conditions as well, require interpreted inferential competence: connect A/B to interventions, explain the relevant symmetry or asymmetry, track the family assumption, and distinguish inference about one fixed device from correlations across changing devices. Do not claim this list is a universal definition. The present structural explanation is stronger than an answer-transfer test alone.

The draft's discontinuity reply is weak. Learning one final bit can be philosophically decisive; discontinuity is not by itself absurd. Replace “most of the inferential structure” with an explicit independent reason: the investigator already knows an actual structural property with counterfactual implications, although they cannot locate the controlling input. This supports partial understanding without assuming that all learning must add understanding continuously.

## Priority 2: make the opponent and contribution exact

No cited theory is shown to reduce understanding to separate intervention answers. The first paragraph's inventory picture is intuitive, but rejecting it may be a lesson for assessment and communication rather than a challenge to an existing philosophical theory. Frigg and Nguyen permit an object of understanding that is itself a relation; their framework therefore need not be modified to recognize this example. The draft concedes this in §5, which is correct but arrives late.

Move that concession into the thesis framing. State that the essay supplies a worked adequacy constraint and a diagnostic example for a restricted assessment practice, not a counterexample to stability accounts. Explain why the restriction is independently tempting: an instructor or reporting format can record every fixed-input result while omitting the competence to reason across possible results. The educational scenario is presently hypothetical; do not imply empirical prevalence without evidence.

A useful exact statement is: two summaries may agree on every possible response to each question in a fixed intervention family, while only one preserves the warranted conditional consequences of discoveries within that family. That is the established representational result. Why retaining those consequences matters for understanding is the philosophical argument. Separating these avoids presenting elementary joint-versus-projection mathematics as a new theory.

## Priority 3: specify the object understood and avoid excess “mechanism” claims

There are three objects in play: why Y=1 occurred, the device's response dependence, and which experiments discriminate hypotheses. The example most convincingly establishes partial understanding of response dependence. Its implications for understanding the actual occurrence should be presented as a further, contrast-relative application. At X=Z=1 the two families do not provide competing complete explanations; they supply constraints on potential explanations. Say clearly whether that itself counts as an incomplete why-answer and defend it with the asymmetry argument.

Moreover, a complete truth table does not determine a concrete internal mechanism. Many implementations can realize Y=X. Calling the last model-selection bit a completion of the explanation overstates what has been learned unless “complete” is explicitly relative to these intervention questions and this functional level. Replace phrases about fully completing understanding with completion of the selected response profile. Preserve the good distinction between variation of a hypothesis and intervention on a fixed target.

## Further objections and precise repairs

- In §4, “not simply ... fewer facts” is misleading: the compressed table omits the fact that A and B differ (or agree). The contribution is that no separate outcome range reveals this omission, not that information disappeared without losing facts. Similarly the investigators' uncertainty is the same only marginally, not the same overall. Qualify these expressions consistently, including the introduction.
- The construction premise is strong enough to carry the causal exclusions. That is acceptable for a counterexample, but not evidence that comparing models independently warrants those exclusions. The draft already largely handles this. Give one sentence specifying that a trustworthy schematic can justify a family before its consequences are understood; no real-world case need be fabricated.
- The same-device stability assumption is essential. If each test reselects X versus Z, A no longer predicts B even though each test separately uses a selector. Explicitly contrast this failure with the successful transfer. This would illuminate what the investigator must understand, rather than merely adding another caveat.
- State that grouping models by complete profiles preserves only the stipulated intervention inquiry. It can discard temporal behavior, internal pathways or other causal questions. The existing qualification is sound; retain it through the conclusion.

## Significance and originality

Significance is moderate but defensible: the essay gives a clean reason why apparently lossless summaries and direct-response tests may fail to preserve or detect causal competence. It is less successful as a general account of explanatory understanding. Narrowing the advertised result would improve force rather than diminish it.

Originality remains uncertain. I found no exact duplicate during focused searches, but neither the author nor this review has established that the philosophical lesson is absent from work on modal understanding, grasping relations, scientific model spaces or potential-response profiles. The cited literature does not establish a gap. Avoid claiming a newly discovered kind of understanding. Claim the argument and diagnostic example provisionally, and distinguish them from their standard formal basis. A small targeted engagement with a primary account of understanding as inferential competence would be more valuable than an expanded generic bibliography.

## Source accuracy and retrieval limits

Pearl's Definition2 in §3.2.1, printed p.17, supports the model-agreement attribution, and the intervention substitution is on printed p.16: https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~kaoru/r355-corrections.pdf . The draft's general attribution is accurate. Do not let vector identification imply that marginal interventional distributions identify a joint cross-world distribution; the present example concerns deterministic complete profiles under stronger assumptions.

Frigg and Nguyen's §§4.1 and5.1–5.3 support the descriptions of stability and relevant/irrelevant features: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-025-02384-y . They already permit richer objects of understanding than isolated responses. This makes the draft's explicit compatibility concession essential.

Weisberg's publisher abstract supports the deliberately narrow common-predictions/theorems attribution, and the citation's 2006 volume/pages are correct despite the platform's later online migration date: https://doi.org/10.1086/518628 . I did not read the full article.

No source quotation, factual fabrication or arithmetic error was detected in the checked passages. The review does not certify originality, philosophical truth or eligibility.

## Readability

The draft is clear, economical and largely jargon-free. The device earns its space, and the table is effective. The opening “same uncertainty” should say “same separate ranges of uncertainty.” Introduce response profile as the complete list of responses before discussing projections; the existing §4 mostly does this well. Move the strongest scope concession forward, then shorten repeated assurances that the result is partial and not mathematically novel. The fourth objection is less urgent than the lookup-card/stable-device objection; replacing it could deepen the argument without lengthening the essay. No decorative scene or longer manuscript is needed.
