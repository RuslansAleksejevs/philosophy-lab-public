# Source and novelty assessment

The relevant primary sources and exact accessible locators are recorded in sources/retrieval-notes.md. Public source text was read through web PDF/HTML extraction; compliant paraphrase notes are retained rather than full copyrighted works. Retrieval timestamps are recorded there.

The proposed broad novelty claim fails: Adams explicitly discusses proving the wrong formal statement and advocates audits including definitions. The current math-commons conventions also already propose tests of definitions and nondegeneracy. Avigad supplies a philosophical background concerning recognition of logical structure, not an opponent who identifies understanding with verification.

Revised candidate: a reason to distinguish accumulation of derivability evidence from discrimination between rival interpretations. Given two plausible readings compatible with a checked certificate but differing in a mathematically relevant consequence, repetition of checks insensitive to their difference cannot resolve the interpretive issue. A contrast directed at that difference can. This yields a conditional duty to answer salient, consequential interpretive challenges, rather than a universal demand that every theorem carry a test suite. The author will demonstrate the distinction using pointwise versus uniform convergence, explicitly defining a mistaken uniform predicate whose theorem can be true while its intended export is false.

Limitations: focused literature coverage, no effectiveness study, no formal mechanized example, and only a modest claim to an original synthesis and worked philosophical argument. Ordinary competence and justified testimony can satisfy the duty; formal proof alone is not being rejected.
