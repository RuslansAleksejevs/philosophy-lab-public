# Sources and candidate refinement

The primary-source retrieval record is sources/reading-notes.md, with exact URLs, dates and locators. Sources were accessed as web-extracted passages, not full-book reading. No long copyrighted excerpts are retained.

The initial general proposal is substantially anticipated by meaningful human control and especially operator contestability. Do not claim to originate the need for informed and effective intervention, collective review, or responsibility matched to control.

Refined contribution to attempt: technologies can preserve correction relative to a specified standard while foreclosing discovery that the standard itself misdescribes the task. Skilled making supplies a case where this matters even without wrongdoing, malfunction or a pre-existing fully articulated objection. Develop a distinction between correcting a result and keeping the specification answerable to experience; explain why this requires a route for exploratory variation rather than merely an appeal channel requiring a settled defeater. Defend a limited norm applying when someone retains authority to determine what counts as good work, rather than a universal duty of manual skill. Compare a hypothetical two-version automated joinery system with equal output reliability and distinguish conditional authority from merely delegated execution.

Originality is TENTATIVE and bounded by focused reading. Fresh criticism should test whether this is a substantive refinement of existing contestability rather than vocabulary alone.
