# Question — essay 25

When does making a tool easier to use undermine the agency it is supposed to extend?

Working thesis (TENTATIVE): The relevant boundary is not loss of manual skill or dependence as such. A technology undermines a user's practical authority when it preserves apparent choice while removing feasible ways to discover and correct mismatches between its operative standards and the user's purpose. Skilled making suggests a positive design norm: correction must remain possible at the level at which the user is held responsible. This may be supplied by individual skill, collective repair or accountable delegation, so it does not require everyone to understand every mechanism.

The essay will distinguish successful output, control of execution and authority over standards; test the account against benign automation, craft tools and opaque systems; and investigate nearby accounts of skill, automation and technological mediation before claiming a contribution. No novelty established yet.
