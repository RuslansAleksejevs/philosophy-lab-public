# Separate critique of essay 30

## Verdict and reconstruction

The manuscript is clear, philosophically sensible, and substantially better than a generic defense of animal intelligence. Its strongest result is a scope constraint: demonstrating that a task admits a non-capacity explanation, identifying what controls a particular response, and denying possession of a capacity are distinct achievements. An associative organization might implement the target capacity; alternatively, it might control performance while an unused capacity remains. The H/R/HR example usefully separates these alternatives. The proposal to test independently constrained expression conditions gives both sides obligations and avoids treating all failures as unfair tests.

Revision is needed chiefly because the mathematical example establishes a narrower evidential result than some prose implies, and the positive opportunity proposal still moves too quickly from rejection of a specified expression hypothesis to non-possession. The contribution is a potentially useful synthesis and diagnostic, not yet a clearly novel general account.

## 1. Pairwise indistinguishability is not evidential neutrality about possession

The exact likelihood equality between H and one masked HR is correct under the stipulated conditions. But the essay moves between this pairwise result and the broader suggestion that increasing evidence of habitual control leaves capacity attribution untouched. These are not equivalent.

Consider prior probabilities P(H)=.2, P(R)=.4, P(HR)=.4, with C possessed by R and HR. Suppose insensitive responding has likelihood 1 under H and HR, but .1 under R. Initially P(C)=.8. After that observation it is .44/.64=.6875. After enough insensitive observations eliminate R, it approaches .4/.6, approximately .667. H and HR retain the same odds, yet the evidence has lowered the probability of possession. This is ordinary model averaging; no privileged skeptical default is required. Conversely a tiny prior for masked HR can yield strong negative evidence about C even though its likelihood equals H's.

The draft acknowledges priors, but chiefly as a simplicity preference between H and HR. It needs to acknowledge that the likelihood of the composite capacity hypothesis also depends on how its supported possible modes of expression are distributed. A surviving masked alternative blocks entailment, not necessarily evidential support for absence.

Precise repair: retain the equality result and explicitly say that no further observations in this domain discriminate H from this HR, whereas they can lower support for possession by disfavoring other capacity-bearing models. Replace broad “untouched” formulations with this conditional claim. This strengthens the essay by distinguishing a real limitation on data from a blanket prohibition the author does not intend.

## 2. Failed opportunities primarily reject a conjunction

The proposed test of sharpened HR involves C, a particular masking mechanism, a release cue, preservation of memory and motivation, and a predicted expression probability. Failure bears directly on that conjunction. Independent evidence for food discrimination and motor ability is helpful but does not establish that the selected cue should release this particular animal's retained capacity. A rival HR2 might retain the same C with a different allocation rule.

The draft already says masks must earn credibility, but the sentence concluding that the animal probably lacks C “within a specified domain” risks changing the subject. If C is the animal's general capacity, a domain-limited failure concerns expression there. If C itself is defined as the capacity to perform within D, the inference becomes easier partly because the target was narrowed. Both targets are legitimate; distinguish them explicitly.

Precise repair: state the two conclusions separately. First, repeated failures can undermine a specified capacity-and-expression hypothesis. Second, a denial of possession requires additional grounds that the tested opportunities cover a substantial share of the independently credible expression hypotheses, or grounds excluding enabling architecture. Mere preregistration supplies transparency, not credibility: a before-the-fact arbitrary cue prediction is still weak. Replace “or explicit commitments made before” with language requiring independently defensible commitments whose evidential strength is reported.

## 3. Non-exercise needs a graded causal interpretation

The binary bypass example is clean, but successful habitual prediction need not mean an outcome-sensitive process made no causal contribution. It might weakly influence choice, lose a competition, or affect latency while leaving response frequency essentially unchanged. Thus data favoring habit dominance can coexist with some exercise, not only hidden possession.

This does not refute the example, which openly stipulates routing. It limits the scope of its application. Add one paragraph separating exclusive control from dominant control and noting that tests may identify a bound on influence rather than categorical non-exercise. This would make the first evidential bridge as careful as the second.

## 4. Originality and significance

Buckner's model/exclusion distinction is a direct precedent, correctly acknowledged. The independently retrieved author manuscript text at p.13 supports that attribution: https://cameronbuckner.net/professional/twoapproaches.pdf (indexed passage; direct open failed). Dacey's holistic framework already requires arguments linking experimental results to substantive models and allows negative findings: https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/714799, §§4, 6–7. The manuscript should not imply that his sensible model comparison ignores model content; present the proposed distinction as a more specific implementation of that framework.

The positive proposal also overlaps closely with Brown and Key's epistemic coverage and context-sensitive account of absence evidence: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11229-020-02961-0, §2 and conclusion. They should be engaged as predecessors to the opportunity requirement, rather than mainly as a warning against multiple-realization defenses. The author's synthesis of constitution, causal allocation, and temporal preservation may still be worthwhile, especially if the pairwise/composite distinction above becomes explicit. A short search cannot establish novelty.

The stakes are real: interpreting a negative result changes what experiment is worth running next. To demonstrate that significance, the revision should give one compact worked decision: if associative implementation is disputed, inspect causal organization; if retained control is disputed, test a justified release condition; if only a local expression model fails, withhold a broader denial unless coverage warrants it. Avoid claiming a new theory of representation, which this manuscript deliberately leaves open.

## 5. Source accuracy and empirical precision

The load-bearing source descriptions are broadly accurate within bounded checks. Dickinson's §3(a) distinguishes devaluation of response-contingent from noncontingent food, not simply suppression after devaluing food: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3427555/ (indexed section read; direct open CAPTCHA). Add that control in the essay's concise paradigm description to explain what selective means. This matters because general aversion or motivational suppression is not the target flexibility.

Bouton's “Effects of context change” supplies a stronger and more specific anchor than the currently cited abstract/introduction: https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13420-021-00488-z. It reports return of outcome sensitivity after context switches and interprets habit as context-dependent interference. Cite that section, preserving the qualification that these findings concern the studied preparations. They substantiate the possibility of retention; they do not independently prove the essay's stipulated two-box architecture. Underlying experiment reports were not independently checked.

Dacey's publisher metadata supplies missing pages 291–312. No source fabrication found. Starzak/Gray is background rather than a premise and was not independently rechecked. Detailed access boundaries and a mistaken unused PMC URL are recorded in sources/critic/retrieval-notes.md.

## 6. Readability and revision priorities

The opening immediately makes the question intelligible; the examples are economical. H, R, and HR are manageable, and the prose explains constitution and allocation before relying on them. The “Is this only familiar experimental caution?” section repeats several earlier claims and can be shortened to make room for the important model-averaging qualification. The claim of “independently constrained opportunities” needs one crisp definition, not more terminology.

Priority order: (1) distinguish fixed-pair likelihood equality from updating about composite possession; (2) specify what opportunity failure actually denies and what further coverage supports non-possession; (3) acknowledge graded causal influence; (4) tighten originality positioning and empirical controls. Preserve the modest, conditional character of the thesis. These changes can improve the argument without expanding it into a literature survey or an inflated essay.
