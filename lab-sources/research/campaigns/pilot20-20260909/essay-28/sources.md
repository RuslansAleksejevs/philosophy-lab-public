# Novelty correction
Initial question about permission to leave coexisting with notice is already substantially anticipated. Alonso expressly offers warning/performance/compensation; Löhr expressly grounds notification in respect. Gilbert expressly allows permissive background understandings. None should be portrayed as overlooking exit or notification. Revised target within the question: a criterion for adequate notice under authorized unilateral exit, distinguishing completion of communicative responsibility from achieved awareness, and identifying receivers' inability to prolong that responsibility by avoidable refusal to attend. Claim is a proposed specification compatible with respect/reliance accounts, not a new foundational theory of shared intention. Search was focused, not exhaustive; priority remains unestablished.
