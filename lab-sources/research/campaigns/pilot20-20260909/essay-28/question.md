# Question
When participants deliberately make a joint undertaking easy for each other to leave, what—if anything—do they still owe one another as participants?

Investigate whether the authority to end joint action and the responsibility to communicate its ending can be separated. Tentative thesis: where a participant has knowingly become a coordination signal on which others appropriately rely, unilateral permission to exit can coexist with a directed duty to make exit legible; this duty is limited by practical opportunity and cannot be converted into a duty to remain. The task is to explain its grounds, boundaries and relation to theories of shared intention and joint commitment, rather than merely restate a general duty against harm.
