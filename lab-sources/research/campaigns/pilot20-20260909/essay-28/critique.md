# Critique of essay 28

## Reconstruction and verdict

The essay defends a directed duty, within voluntary cooperation with an already established unilateral exit entitlement, to make termination reasonably accessible to affected partners. It distinguishes permission to stop, adequate communication, and actual awareness. Accepted participation sustains practical reliance; privileged access to one's own decision and respect for partners' agency ground a duty to correct the resulting informational mismatch. An opportunity to update, rather than guaranteed uptake, is the proposed object of that duty.

Verdict: revise. The focused question, honest positioning and largely clear exposition are strengths. The adequacy criterion is plausible. Its central defense currently conflates an outstanding communicative duty with a veto over participation, and equivocates between objective accessibility and reasonable care. These are repairable but load-bearing problems. Originality remains modest and unestablished: the candidate contribution is a specification of discharge, not notification without permission.

## 1. A notice duty is not a participation veto

The essay correctly insists that the reader may stop even if notice remains defective. But it then says requiring actual awareness transfers equivalent control over continued participation to the editor. This does not follow. An outcome-based theorist can consistently say: stop reading now, retain a duty to communicate, and suspend or excuse further efforts when impossible or disproportionate. The continuing duty concerns communication, exactly as the essay's own initial distinction allows. Its existence does not revive reading.

The real objection is therefore excessive control over the duration or burden of communicative responsibility. Argue against that burden directly, without treating it as authority over participation. In §§1, 4 and 7 replace claims of an equivalent veto with this narrower claim. Then confront the strongest rival: a duty to secure understanding subject to reasonable-effort limits, recipient obstruction, and impossibility. Why does describing this as reasonable opportunity have different implications, rather than redescribing its excuses? A useful answer would identify whether blameless nonuptake leaves an unfulfilled duty, a remedial claim, or neither. The present essay says it is fulfillment, but needs an argument for that classification.

Deliberate refusal also does not refute all outcome standards. A recipient may waive or forfeit a claim to further efforts without receiving knowledge. Treat obstruction as one test case, not a general proof that success requirements are illegitimate.

## 2. Frozen-screen case exposes two different standards

The defined duty requires making correction reasonably available. In the frozen-screen case the recipient has no actual access, even under entirely appropriate attention. The writer nevertheless calls the duty discharged because the sender reasonably believed delivery worked. That is reasonable care toward providing access, not necessarily actual provision of access.

Choose explicitly between an evidence-relative duty of reasonable efforts and an objective opportunity duty whose breach can be blameless. An actual opportunity can exist without awareness, but does not exist merely because the sender could not foresee its absence. This distinction matters for the promised contribution about completion. One repair is: the participant owes reasonable, evidence-sensitive steps directed at providing timely access, with renewed requirements upon new evidence. A second is: access is owed but justified failure leaves no blame and only proportionate remedial duties. The latter preserves an outcome element. Explain why the preferred account improves on the rival. Do not silently use both.

## 3. Ground and scope need a sharper defeater

Section 2 convincingly identifies who bears informational costs but moves quickly from cheap correction to a directed obligation. Consider two volunteers who explicitly agree that no departure notice is expected, with either free to check whether the other remains. They still knowingly sustain reliance while participating. Is silent departure permissible? Presumably yes: the partner has accepted the informational risk. Add this case to show that permission to exit and waiver of notice are distinct; specify that the proposed default applies where notice has not been waived and the relevant reliance remains warranted by accepted arrangements.

More generally, what does accepting reliance mean beyond knowingly participating? Mere foreseeability cannot suffice: someone may reasonably plan around a stranger's visible habits without gaining a right to updates. The essay already gestures at role acceptance, but should make reciprocal, mutually recognized coordination the operative distinction and admit that the grounding remains a moral premise about responsibility for supported practical orientation.

## 4. Temporal adequacy and new duties

Accessibility needs a time dimension. A conspicuous shared-document notice checked only tomorrow is useless to the editor who must decide now, even if the channel is established. Specify access early enough for the practical revision presently at stake, subject to proportionate cost. Clarify that this can require announcing cessation simultaneously with stopping, without requiring a notice period of continued performance.

The renewed-duty proposal is promising. But describe what persists between discharge and later discovery: a standing susceptibility to new correction duties arising from one's role in the error, or an ongoing duty with episodic demands? Either works; calling it a new duty alone does not settle the explanatory question. The difference need not become a separate metaphysical excursus.

## 5. Harmlessness and remedy

The harmless editor example is intelligible but its stipulation that every opportunity is identical sits awkwardly beside the claim that an opportunity was denied. Distinguish unchanged external options and behavior from the lost opportunity to deliberate on accurate information. Defend why that informational opportunity has value in this relationship even when all outcomes are unchanged. Otherwise the example partly stipulates away the very interest the explanation invokes. Present the residual complaint as a defended judgment, not decisive consensus.

The categorical remedial sentence is too strong: a remedy need not reproduce exactly the protected interest. Restitution or compensation can use other goods. More reading might be voluntary compensation or owed under an independently justified remedial principle without retrospectively showing that original continued participation was secured. Preserve the narrower claim that notice violation alone does not automatically establish an entitlement to resumed performance.

## Significance and originality

An account of when communication is complete has practical and philosophical value, especially when cooperation is deliberately easy to leave. The distinction between original completion and renewed duties is the most promising development. Yet reasonable notice and proportionate efforts are familiar normative forms. The essay needs to show what its joint-action analysis adds: perhaps how reliance fixes the audience, urgency and protected content while reciprocal agency bounds responsibility. No broad claim of a new normative category is warranted.

Focused novelty searches did not verify a close precursor for this exact criterion; they did not establish its absence. The prior work already supplies most of the ground. Keep the explicit admission that this is a specification within existing respect/reliance approaches. Resolve the strongest conceptual rival before expanding the bibliography.

## Readability and precise revision priorities

The opening makes the issue immediately intelligible. The volunteer example earns its space. “Accessible correction” is explained and need not be replaced, but “practical orientation,” “directedness,” and “uptake” can usually become plainer language. Sections 3–4 and 7 repeat the notice/awareness contrast enough that space can be reclaimed for the two principal objections.

Revision order: (1) separate freedom to stop work from freedom to end communicative efforts; (2) settle reasonable care versus actual accessible opportunity using the frozen screen; (3) answer the qualified outcome-duty rival; (4) add waiver and temporal adequacy; (5) qualify the remedial claim and clarify the harmless case; (6) trim repeated restatement. Retain the narrow scope and the source-positioning candor. No final truth, originality, eligibility or judge-fidelity certification is offered.
