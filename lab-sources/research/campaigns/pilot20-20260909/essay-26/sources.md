# Source stage
Primary sources and actual access boundaries are preserved in sources/reading-notes.md. Three focused sources establish the closest precedents: Carel on bodily doubt, Wendell on fluctuating capacity and accommodation, Makowski on planning plasticity. Source URLs, retrieval time, locators and limitations are in that file.

The initial broad thesis that unreliable bodily capacity damages planning is substantially anticipated. The narrower contribution to develop is a distinction between stability of performance and security of permission to revise: bodily agency can be restored by institutional changes to the consequences of interruption, with bodily probabilities unchanged. The proposed argument will isolate this through matched hypothetical cases and defend an agency-sensitive criterion against productivity, generic risk, and over-accommodation objections. This is a candidate conceptual contribution, not an established novelty claim.

No exhaustive literature search or complete reading of the books linked by search results is claimed. The essay will use original argument and short paraphrases, not extended source reproduction.
