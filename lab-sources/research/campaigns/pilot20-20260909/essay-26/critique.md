# Critique: A Body One Can Plan With

Fresh separate AI critic, essay 26 only. 2026-09-10 UTC. Recommendation: REVISE. The essay is clear and defensible as a modest conceptual refinement. Its present evidence supports less originality than its promising central mechanism could earn with sharper cases.

## Strongest reconstruction

Future participation can require deciding before one knows whether bodily capacity will be available. Such decisions expose people to consequences if participation fails. An arrangement that supplies feasible, authorized and bearable revision can enlarge the undertakings a person may reasonably enter without changing bodily performance. The improvement concerns governance of activity, not merely output or relief. Crucially, a usable revision route must work in the bodily circumstances that necessitate it. This last condition prevents treating the person as an always-capable manager of an intermittently capable body.

The account succeeds in distinguishing bodily predictability from social terms of participation. It also correctly separates provision of an outcome from authority over one's contribution. Its strongest potential contribution is a constraint on accommodation: assess the availability of repair conditionally on the state that triggers repair, not on the person's usual capacities. That is more precise than the familiar claim that flexible participation matters.

## Main vulnerabilities and concrete repairs

1. **The matched case changes several goods at once.** The first group excludes Lea after cancellation, whereas the second preserves later opportunities and provides a substitute contribution. A critic can explain the improvement entirely as a larger set of future opportunities and protection from exclusion. Holding expected attendance fixed does not hold relevant performance opportunities or output fixed: a prewritten contribution adds another performance channel. The point survives, but the existing example does not isolate it. Add a comparison with identical continued membership, same substitute, same attendance and same contribution. Vary only whether Lea can activate the substitute on previously agreed terms or must secure a discretionary exception while ill. Explain separately why secure authorization differs from a favorable probability of permission. Alternatively describe the initial case candidly as an illustration of several related benefits, not an experiment isolating one.

2. **Commitment is equivocal.** Lea may be making an unconditional promise followed by excused breach, or undertaking a conditional plan whose alternative performance was accepted from the start. The latter contains no revision of a commitment at all: she executes its contingency clause. The manuscript moves between intending, reserving, promising, continuing membership and directing a life. This matters because the title claim may be about predictable contingency arrangements rather than a right to change commitments. Explicitly distinguish a standing project, a scheduled contribution and the agreed obligations to others. Then explain that secure revisability encompasses both advance alternatives and legitimate later alteration, although their normative structures differ. Do not imply that unilateral intention revision releases interpersonal obligations.

3. **The definition risks redescribing the desired judgment.** Burdens one can reasonably accept and an undertaking one can reasonably enter make the comparative test close to: better terms improve agency when they improve agency. Supply a more discriminating failure condition. For example, an alternative available only through a task that the triggering episode foreseeably prevents does not supply the advertised access, even if it is cheap on average and formally authorized. Distinguish feasibility in relevant states, the participant's retained authority, and justified allocation of burdens. This would make the concept useful for detecting failures rather than simply naming successful accommodations.

4. **Accessible activation can conflict with retained authority.** Suppose Lea cannot send even a one-word cancellation during an episode. Automatic substitution solves that problem, but an organizer may incorrectly infer incapacity and replace her when she wants to participate. Requiring contemporaneous authorization recreates the access failure; unconditional automation can remove control. This is the hardest objection generated by the essay's own best insight. Develop a feasible response: previously authorized triggers, a default that preserves later correction, a trusted delegate, or a low-demand veto where possible. Acknowledge cases in which no arrangement perfectly preserves both values. Prior authorization counts as agency only with adequate scope and opportunity for later revision; it is not blanket license for paternalism.

5. **Secure revision is not always agency-enhancing.** A person can deliberately choose a demanding undertaking whose meaning partly depends on making herself accountable: a friend relies on an agreed visit, or an ensemble relies on a performance. Costly withdrawal may enable cooperation rather than trap the agent. The current burden and essential-activity qualifications gesture toward this but do not fully meet it. State that an assured exit is a dimension of some opportunities, not a monotonic rule that easier exit improves all agency. Show why the relevant bodily contingencies fall within an agreed exception without erasing what makes the undertaking a commitment.

6. **The embodiment claim should remain structural, not exclusive.** The train contrast is too convenient: power failures, displacement or caregiving emergencies can also disable both performance and negotiation. The draft largely acknowledges this. Make the decisive distinction explicit: bodily fluctuation is one important source of correlated loss, not the sole source. The contribution is the requirement to model the reviser's actual capacities in the contingency, rather than a unique metaphysical category of embodied risk.

## Significance and originality

The subject matters: counting successful participation can hide who controls entry, interruption and return. The manuscript also presents a productive alternative to measuring accommodation solely by output. However, naming an intuitively good arrangement is not yet a major philosophical contribution. The explicit condition on repair capacity, combined with the authorization conflict above, could yield an argument with a clearer consequence that a reader could previously have missed.

Wendell is very close, and the essay rightly admits this. Her text already couples variable capacity, alternative contributions and judgments of commitment. It even places inability to communicate beside inability to attend. Thus the correlated-capacity point should be presented as an explicit diagnostic development of a known problem, with novelty provisional, rather than as an unprecedented discovery. [Wendell, “Accommodating Chronic Illnesses,” printed pp.167–168](https://ieas-szeged.hu/downtherabbithole/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/Lennard-J.-Davis-ed.-The-Disability-Studies-Reader-Routledge-2014.pdf).

A brief engagement with crip-time writing would strengthen the account's position. Samuels's opening and writing-time discussion describe flexible temporal arrangements alongside enduring loss. This reinforces the essay's refusal to identify accommodation with full restoration. The precise advance-authorization account is not established there by the passages I read. [Samuels, “Six Ways of Looking at Crip Time,” opening and writing-time passages, reproduction](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/62d81c97af97107399ce5b10/t/62e047ae1b277b677f25e1f6/1658865583159/Six%2BWays%2Bof%2BLooking%2Bat%2BCrip%2BTime%2B_%2BDisability%2BStudies%2BQuarterly.pdf). No exhaustive originality judgment is warranted.

## Readability and revision priorities

The opening question, example and progression are unusually easy to follow. Preserve that economy. “Standing to direct the project” is the main abstraction needing translation: specify whether Lea chooses the content, activates a substitute, keeps membership, or schedules return. “Commitment,” “authority” and “revision” should not each cover all these functions without distinction. The gift example is less useful than the automatic-substitution conflict and could make room for it. The literature section can remain short.

Prioritize: clarify the object of commitment; strengthen the comparison; make contingency-specific usability the argumentative center; address authorization when capacity to authorize fails; retain modest originality claims and the existing limits on accommodation. These are substantive revisions, not requests for length or decorative prose.
