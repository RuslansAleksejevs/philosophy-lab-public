# Sources stage
The independently chosen question encounters a direct precedent in Maja Spener (2022), especially sections 2.2 and 3. The general claim that introspection can alter its target, and that accurate reports of altered experience need extra support to describe unobserved experience, is not an original contribution here.

The draft will develop a narrower proposal: evidence about introspective change need not establish complete preservation; one-directional constraints on production or loss of a specified feature license different positive and negative retrospective inferences. Repeated introspection may stabilize a changed target without validating the first transition. These are proposed deductions, not established empirical laws.

Primary works actually used: Spener (2022), Sergent and Rees (2007), and selected portions of Schwitzgebel (2008). Exact bibliography, URLs, accessible locators, source claims and gaps are preserved in sources/retrieval-notes.md. No study was performed, and no primary experiments were independently reanalysed. Broader novelty remains uncertain.
