# Question
When attention to experience changes the experience being examined, what can an introspective report warrant about the experience before attention?

Tentative thesis: a report can be first-person authoritative concerning its present target while failing to identify the earlier target. Moving from the former to the latter requires a bridge premise about preservation of the relevant phenomenal property under the introspective intervention. This creates a local, feature-relative limit rather than global skepticism about introspection. Develop the strongest version of this requirement and investigate whether it adds anything to existing discussions of introspective disturbance and phenomenal overflow.
