Revised contribution to investigate in the draft: strengthen the established local-scarcity reply by separating compensation from fulfillment. A time-specific obligation to another person can remain decisive before its deadline even when all parties are immortal and arbitrarily many later benefits are available. The argument does not require permanent psychological damage, infinite regret, finite total welfare or finite lifespan. Such obligations support one recognizably meaningful pattern of commitment, but do not guarantee an immortal life is desirable, meaningful overall, or motivationally inexhaustible.
