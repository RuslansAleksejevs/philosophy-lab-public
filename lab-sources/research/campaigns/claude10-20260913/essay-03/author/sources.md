All retrievals were made on 2026-09-13 by WebSearch/WebFetch from this task session. For each WebFetch, a separate direct HTTP retrieval was performed and archived locally; the paths below point to those archives. Coverage is reported honestly: "full" means the complete work's text was available and read; "excerpt" means a bounded portion was available and read; "summary" means only abstract/listing-level information was obtained; "unavailable" means no text was extractable.

I therefore did **not** quote it, did not cite a page for it, and did not build any argument on it; the essay refers to Scheler only for what pp. 33–37 support, plus a general (hedged) statement that ST agrees with him that the past is in a restricted respect open.
