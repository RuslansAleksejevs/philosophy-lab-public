## Revised contribution target after research

The first hypothesis, taken broadly, is substantially anticipated by induced-reliance and retraction literature. Preserve that failure rather than relabel it as novelty. The draft will instead defend a constraint on using authoritative clarification to adjudicate earlier audience conduct: the issuer must keep apart correctness about intended content, warrant for pre-clarification conduct, and entitlement to impose consequences. The difficult case involves two reasonable readings that shaped competing participants’ work differently. Honoring every reading is not a sufficient repair because protecting one party may alter the comparison faced by another. A procedural protection can survive without attributing both incompatible contents to one utterance or giving audiences a veto on its true interpretation.

This is a proposed extension and argued application of prior work, not a claim to have discovered the general ethics of repair. The exact contribution, its significance, and the adequacy of its limiting conditions must stand on the draft’s argument.
