## Revised central result

The final makes a conditional claim: an agent can be required to restore an inquiry arrangement despite unchanged rational confidence in the answer, when new information defeats the expected substitute for an independently required remaining inquiry and does not affect the answer's evidential support. The forecast grounding withdrawal must concern the proposed policy, including coordination and anticipated uptake. This is a focused synthesis of instrumental inquiry norms and established coverage conditions. It is not a new logical phenomenon or a demonstrated historical first.

## Response to review 1

**1. Contribution beyond the combined predecessor. Accepted in substance.** The final explicitly reconstructs the strongest combined predecessor: instrumental appraisal of inquiry together with tracking, transmission, receipt, and timing conditions. It grants that a sophisticated version can derive the result. I removed the implication that ordinary means–end reasoning leaves an irreducible residual problem. The main contribution is now the exact diagnosis of required inquiry repair without belief revision. A new departure-triggered replacement comparison develops the prospective assessment: a joint release can activate a reliable reserve, or leave a reserve idle because its manual activation was never assigned. Present coverage and present reserve competence do not settle that transition. This supplies a useful application while leaving historical originality uncertain.

I do not adopt an inference from derivability to philosophical worthlessness. The review itself did not require that inference. A precise synthesis can still contribute by showing which rational response addresses a failed inquiry arrangement. The final offers that limited contribution and leaves its competitive significance open.

**2. Evidence-relative reason versus actual survival. Accepted.** The rule now specifies the agent, the decision time, the available grounds, and the adopted policy. It distinguishes the reason possessed by an individual, support for a joint proposal, and actual performance. It compares an adequately forecast ninety-nine-percent replacement that unexpectedly fails with a cancellation known before departure. A permissible risk's unlucky realization does not retroactively defeat the original reason. The discussion of lotteries concerns actual coordination rules and anticipated uptake, rather than the conjunction of every permitted individual action. Universal-sounding claims about the actual last investigator have been removed. The final no longer makes actual causal survival necessary for an evidence-relative justification.

**3. Invalid bridge from lost stopping reason to required resumption. Accepted.** The original opening's claims that checking was worthwhile and faced no unreasonable cost did not establish an obligation. The final explicitly separates the necessary additional premise: an undefeated requirement to complete a residual inquiry, with personal inspection or an adequate substitute necessary to meeting it. The opening now illustrates the failure of the offered reason alone; the later cancellation case adds that premise before deriving required repair. Two contrasts cover optional improvement and permissible abandonment. The same stopping reason can fail in all three cases, with required resumption following only in the first. The final does not derive obligations from mere curiosity, usefulness, or an arbitrary intention.

## Response to review 2

**1. Forecast support versus adequacy of forecasted performance. Accepted.** The final splits the justification into two judgments. The withdrawal condition constrains the evidentially supported forecast under the proposed policy. An independent inquiry standard determines whether that performance makes the withdrawn effort dispensable. A single well-supported ninety-percent detection forecast passes an illustrative eighty-five-percent target and fails an illustrative ninety-five-percent target. These are expressly conditional examples, not universal standards or thresholds defined in terms of the permissibility of stopping. The condition is necessary for the target justification and remains insufficient for full permission.

**2. Unchanged-confidence timeline. Accepted.** The final gives one coherent sequence. At noon Noor has ninety-percent confidence and expects a later check; the established reporting rule permits no result before five. At one she learns of a cancellation generated by an administrative random procedure she has grounds to regard as independent of the date and the first reading. No evidentially significant silence has yet occurred. The proper confidence remains unchanged across the cancellation notice, while the forecast supporting delegated inquiry changes. This avoids relying on a failure to update. A separate later-discovery sequence explicitly has confidence rise to 90/91 after informative silence and return to 90/100 after the cancellation is learned. The final distinguishes no immediate reduction from no reduction below the original baseline. Independence is attributed to the agent's supported information as well as stipulated in the case.

**3. Composition versus conditional planning; individual reason versus arrangement success. Accepted.** The final opening uses the informed coordinator to establish a clean justificatory failure. It then treats decentralized reasonable mistakes as a different case, potentially involving no individual irrationality. It denies that conditional permissions mysteriously contradict one another. Its principal result is the conditional distinction between belief correction and inquiry repair. The final concedes that an adequate combined predecessor yields the same verdicts and states that the development makes a transition and its repair explicit. No claim that a new logical law has been discovered remains.
