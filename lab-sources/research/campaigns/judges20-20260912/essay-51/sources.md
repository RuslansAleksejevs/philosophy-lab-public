## Implications for the contribution

The initial unrestricted correction-route proposal would risk overstating both novelty and the need for noncircular justification. Existing work already explains absent coverage and passing verification to others. The contribution will instead derive a prospective condition: the expectation of corrective evidence invoked to justify stopping must be assessed with the proposed withdrawals in effect. It will distinguish current support for an answer, informative later silence, and permission to stop; show why separate permissions may fail to compose; and argue that discovering a coverage failure can require repair of inquiry without immediate loss of the independently supported answer. This is offered as a bounded development at the intersection of established discussions, not a new foundational theory or a demonstrated historical first.

Strongest unresolved originality concern: broader work on epistemic free riding or collective inquiry may already state an equivalent transition constraint. Strongest philosophical concern: the proposed condition may be only an application of familiar instrumental rationality. The draft must explain what useful result this application yields rather than claim independence by stipulation.
