## Consequences for the contribution

The general claims that testimony involves responsibilities and that retraction has social conditions already have explicit antecedents. The draft will not offer these as new. Its proposed advance is narrower: defend a prospective, role-sensitive continuity condition for presenting a claim as an institution's current maintained answer; distinguish losing that standing from acquiring evidence that the proposition is false; explain why retiring that endorsement can be the appropriate response before any first-order correction is warranted. The intended argument and fictional cases will be developed after this source boundary succeeds.

Open limitations: incomplete coverage of assertion/retraction literature, group epistemic responsibility, diachronic justification, institutional memory and forgotten evidence. No demonstrated priority over that literature. No empirical evidence of the fictional institutional failure's prevalence. No established quantitative standard for adequate monitoring. Originality is a defended local proposal relative to this reading, not certified novelty.
