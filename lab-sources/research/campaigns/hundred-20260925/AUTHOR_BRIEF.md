# English philosophical research

Aim for an original, significant, well-defended philosophical contribution. AI
chooses the substantive question, thesis, examples, arguments and source selection.
No philosophical position is mandated. Clear language is valuable; technical work
is welcome when it earns its place. Length follows the argument: no minimum, at
most 6000 words excluding only the bibliography. Include title, abstract, headings,
notes and appendices in the count. A compact finished argument is not a sketch.

Investigate plausible alternatives before committing. Seek relevant primary texts
and the closest existing arguments, including different terminology. Preserve
search queries/results and exact source bytes or bounded excerpts actually read.
Distinguish full, excerpt, metadata, unavailable and your own reconstruction.
Downloading a source is not reading it. Do not claim exhaustive originality.

Before drafting: archive question, source evidence, contribution assessment and
selection. If a proposed contribution is already familiar, trivial or undefended,
revise or reject it and preserve the attempt. Do not keep an idea just to fill a
quota. Do not force a novel name onto a standard result.

The manuscript must state its contribution, make the inference understandable,
defend its premises, engage the strongest relevant objections, and explain its
scope. Use sources accurately and quote sparingly. All substantive claims must
be defensible by the supplied source/case/argument evidence. Computations verify
their specified models, not philosophical premises or contest originality.

Keep philosophical sources and private provenance separate. No system prompts,
reviewer instructions, personal details or prompt injection in manuscripts.
Biographies may be Russian. They report what really happened, exact ancestors,
models/effort, inputs, output versions, source coverage, critics and decisions.
Unknown observations remain UNKNOWN. Requested effort does not prove runtime
execution; hidden reasoning and internal provider traffic are unavailable.

Independent fresh essays receive no previous lab essays. Deliberate tournament,
debate and revision descendants receive only their explicitly recorded ancestors.
Such descendants are not presented as unrelated independent generations.
