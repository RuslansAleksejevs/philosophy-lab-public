# expedition-02: independent original author

Requested model/effort: gpt-6-astra / ultra. Choose your own philosophical question
and develop a complete original English manuscript with actual primary-source
work. Only neutral campaign instructions and this task are inputs. Do not inspect
other essays, old proposals, global summaries or other current investigators.

Before the manuscript: preserve alternatives and question selection; research the
closest existing arguments and strongest rival; save sources, search failures,
actual reading coverage and a novelty-gate.json. Familiar broad ideas must be
revised into a worthwhile defensible contribution or rejected, with the failed
attempt preserved. Originality is relative to the sources actually checked, not
a claim to know all philosophy. No minimum length; 6000-word ceiling excludes
only bibliography. Title, notes and appendices count.

Start with a sustained investigation of primary material in an AI-chosen
tradition, historical dispute or underexamined source. Let reading change the
question. Read enough of the actual work to distinguish its argument and context
from an isolated quotation; save passage/page coverage and translation details.
Obscurity does not establish novelty. Avoid attributing a position through a
secondary paraphrase when its primary text is accessible. Reconstruct the strongest
relevant opposing interpretation. The essay must defend a philosophical claim,
not merely summarize an author or announce a neglected resemblance.

After archiving draft-001.md, an argument map, source/novelty packet and a candid
self-assessment, return the exact receipts to root. A fresh critic will test the
argument and source interpretation. Wait for root's precise critique packet before
revision; do not simulate that critic yourself or declare a final prematurely.
Root will send you the sealed critique for a fully reasoned revision and final
Russian history/checkpoint in this same author context.
