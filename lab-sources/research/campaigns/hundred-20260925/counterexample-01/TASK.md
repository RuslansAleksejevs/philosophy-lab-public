# counterexample-01: independent original author

Requested model/effort: gpt-6-astra / ultra. Choose your own philosophical question
and develop a complete original English manuscript with actual primary-source
work. Only neutral campaign instructions and this task are inputs. Do not inspect
other essays, old proposals, global summaries or other current investigators.

Before the manuscript: preserve alternatives and question selection; research the
closest existing arguments and strongest rival; save sources, search failures,
actual reading coverage and a novelty-gate.json. Familiar broad ideas must be
revised into a worthwhile defensible contribution or rejected, with the failed
attempt preserved. Originality is relative to the sources actually checked, not
a claim to know all philosophy. No minimum length; 6000-word ceiling excludes
only bibliography. Title, notes and appendices count.

Choose a consequential philosophical claim whose scope makes counterexample
search informative. State its strongest defensible formulation, its actual source
and all scope conditions before searching. Design cases or models that can expose
a failure or an overlooked distinction. Use executable exhaustive search,
parameter sweeps, optimization or simulation where the mapping to the argument
warrants it. Preserve runnable code, assumptions, seeds/bounds and actual outputs,
including failed searches. A finite check is not a theorem beyond its tested
space; a theorem about the model is not proof that its philosophical interpretation
is correct. Do not force decorative mathematics onto an unsuitable question.

The goal is a philosophical contribution that survives the strongest response:
repair the claim, narrow the objection, or identify the disputed premise. A toy
counterexample to a straw version is a failed attempt, not an automatic result.
Archive the question/source/novelty work, real search artifacts and a complete
English draft. Return those receipts to root for a fresh argument/model critic.
Wait for that exact critique before producing the requested author revision and
final history/checkpoint. Do not simulate an independent critic yourself.

Follow FRESH_DISPATCH_V2.md for archival operations and fresh-context limits.
