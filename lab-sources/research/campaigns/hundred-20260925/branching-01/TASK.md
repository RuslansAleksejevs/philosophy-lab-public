# branching-01: independent original author

Requested model/effort: gpt-6-astra / ultra. Choose your own philosophical question
and develop a complete original English manuscript with actual primary-source
work. Only neutral campaign instructions and this task are inputs. Do not inspect
other essays, old proposals, global summaries or other current investigators.

Before the manuscript: preserve alternatives and question selection; research the
closest existing arguments and strongest rival; save sources, search failures,
actual reading coverage and a novelty-gate.json. Familiar broad ideas must be
revised into a worthwhile defensible contribution or rejected, with the failed
attempt preserved. Originality is relative to the sources actually checked, not
a claim to know all philosophy. No minimum length; 6000-word ceiling excludes
only bibliography. Title, notes and appendices count.

Produce the original D0 for a later comparison of criticism cycles. Do serious
self-examination and primary research, but get no separate critic for D0. Preserve
all own drafts/self-revisions; select and seal one complete D0 manuscript and its
exact source dossier. The quality target is the same as for a final essay, not a
weak draft manufactured to make later criticism look helpful.

Root will create two independent descendants with fresh revisers: one critique
cycle and three critique cycles. You do not participate in those roles or see their
outputs. Return D0, precise source dossier, novelty gate, word count, Russian
history and all receipt IDs. Append the canonical checkpoint with outcome CONTINUE
and explain that the original is sealed for branching, not yet the selected essay.
Do not claim three descendants would count as three completed essays.
