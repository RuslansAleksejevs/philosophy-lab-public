## Exact tournament, debate and branch rules

Tournament generators receive disjoint ID ranges of forty ideas each. Root checks
substantive duplicates after sealing all three pools. Same topic is not itself a
duplicate. Preserve duplicates and any replacement attempts, and report both total
attempts and the final distinct pool size. At 120→24 and 24→6 keep at least one
defensible unusual/disputed candidate eligible for the reserved final place.
Assessment rubric: clarity, argument, significance, originality relative to read
sources, engagement with closest literature, and accuracy. Record individual
reasons/uncertainties; a fatal argument/source defect is not averaged away by style.
Selectors receive exact candidate versions in a fixed recorded order. New arguments
originated by an assessor are separately attributed if later supplied to an author.

Each debate has two sealed independent opening positions. In each of three rounds,
each side independently writes an objection to the other's current position; after
both objections are archived, each writes its reply to the objection addressed to
it. Thus a round has four actual messages, twelve per debate after the two openings.
Round 2/3 uses the same completed previous-round dossier on both sides. Repetition
is not a new argumentative move. Concessions and position changes are permitted.
The final author receives the two openings and all twelve messages, plus sources.

Branching uses truly separate descendants of each sealed original D0: preserve D0
as the zero-cycle version; a fresh author and fresh critic produce D1 in branch A;
another fresh author with three fresh sequential critics produces D3 in branch B.
Both start with the same exact D0/source packet. They cannot read each other's
outputs. Main author/critic settings stay Astra/ultra for this comparison; any
additional differing-model check is separately labeled rather than confounded with
cycle count. A fresh selector initially compares anonymous exact D0/D1/D3 versions
in recorded order before labels are revealed. One idea counts once. Repairs after
selection are distinct stages and do not rewrite the measured earlier versions.

need not win its critic's agreement. Significant unsolved defects require further
