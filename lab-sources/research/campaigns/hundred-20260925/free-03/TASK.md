# Free investigation 03

Executor: one fresh Codex author, gpt-6-astra / ultra. Independently choose any
worthwhile philosophical question and produce one complete English essay. You
have freedom over topic, tradition, method and level of technical exposition.
No other lab essays, global research summaries or sibling sources are inputs.

This is the deliberately uncriticized method: do serious self-examination,
source work and self-revision, but there is no separate critic. Do not invent one.
Do not request a reviewer or reuse another agent's feedback. Quality matters more
than speed; spend effort on alternatives, actual source reading and the strongest
objections, not unnecessary length. There is no minimum word count.

Before drafting, preserve: (1) question.md with alternatives and selected question;
(2) sources.md, source-manifest.json, actual retrieved sources/search results;
(3) novelty-gate.json giving closest prior arguments, what is new, why it matters,
access limits, and decision PASS_RELATIVE/REVISE/REJECT. Archive these stages and
exact selections before relying on them. Retain rejected attempts as separate files.
Then preserve draft-001.md, self-review.md, any numbered revisions, final.md,
word-count.json and a Russian HISTORY.md. The final is a complete scholarly essay,
not an outline, progress report or source summary. Bibliography alone is excluded
from the 6000-word ceiling. Explicitly report unresolved limitations.

Use campaign AUTHOR_BRIEF.md, OPERATIONS.md, competition/RULES_SUMMARY.md and the
hundred-artifacts instructions supplied by root. Never overwrite a sealed file.
After each required archive operation succeeds, continue autonomously to the next
own-author stage. A required archive failure stops dependent research and is
reported to root; a substantive dead end should be retained before trying another
idea in this same independent investigation. Report final path, count and receipts.
