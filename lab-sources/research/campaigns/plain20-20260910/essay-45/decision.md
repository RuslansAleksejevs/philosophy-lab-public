# Decision

Outcome: **REVISE**.

The candidate is retained in revised form. The critique exposed an equivocation between causal explanation and a sufficient reason for a contrast. The final essay now presents a narrower, conditional result: an explanation common to several accepted alternatives cannot by itself establish difference completeness, and moving a contingent split backward does not eliminate it. It explicitly allows stochastic causation, token perturbations, and agent causation as possible explanations.

The result is philosophically useful as an audit of metaphysical completeness claims, but originality is not established and the difference-coverage premise remains disputed. Further work should test the argument against primary contemporary accounts of stochastic explanation, contrastive sufficient reason, and agent causation before any competition selection.
