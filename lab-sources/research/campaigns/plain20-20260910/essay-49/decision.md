# Decision

Outcome: **REVISE**.

The candidate is retained in revised form. The final essay replaces the draft's broad “demand” with a defended bridge principle: when a production process makes specified evidence neutral between serious rivals, support for their shared coarse facts cannot be transferred to the causal or motivational link on which they differ. If that link is central, the historian must provide independent support, defeat the production account, or disclose the link's conjectural status.

The critique was accepted on the central bridge, local and graded neutrality, the threshold for serious alternatives, the distinction between evidential and public-writing norms, conditional background facts, engagement with Malaterre, the Venetian application, and the identified language problems. The empirical application remains schematic because only the recorded sections of the source were consulted. No truth, novelty, independence, or competition-readiness certificate is claimed.
