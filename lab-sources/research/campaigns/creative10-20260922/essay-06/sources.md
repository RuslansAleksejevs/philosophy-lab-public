## Selected claim

See the exact `proposal.md`. A finite acyclic classification preserving every strict dependency exists exactly when grounding paths have a uniform finite length bound. The condition persists for finite refinements of an existing finite taxonomy. Therefore apparent cycles can be unavoidable in all finite charts even where every explanatory chain is finite. The basic order theory is old; the proposed philosophical increment concerns the limits of using taxonomic refinement to diagnose metaphysical cycles.
