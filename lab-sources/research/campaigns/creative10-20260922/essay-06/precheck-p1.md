## Assessment of the increment

A finite quotient DAG bounds every token path; maximum incoming path length provides a finite ranking when paths are uniformly bounded. Pairing that rank with an existing finite partition proves the refinement claim. The disjoint union of finite paths of unbounded lengths has neither token cycles nor infinite paths but defeats every finite strict quotient.

This is modest but more than a new example or name. It changes the quantifier from a misleading coarse description to the impossibility of repair by any finite subdivision. The philosophical consequence is a genuine restriction on a proposed inference: persistent diagrammatic cycles do not logically establish circular grounding or descending infinitism. Even complete finite termination leaves open a global representational obstruction. A familiar theorem can establish that useful restriction; mathematical novelty is unnecessary.

Significance remains conditional. The result constrains complete strict charts, not ordinary lossy diagrams, recursive descriptions, or all theories of kinds.
