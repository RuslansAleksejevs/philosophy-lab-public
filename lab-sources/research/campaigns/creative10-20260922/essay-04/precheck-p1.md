## What survives the independent search

The modest substantive increment is a conditional responsibility standard for a relay: assess its earlier representation choice by whether it preserves the distinctions necessary for later *selective* withdrawal of testimonial support. The two-report example separates original truth and initial justification, accurate subsequent correction, and avoidable destruction of the audience's ability to use the unaffected report. This is more than replacing “database” with “speaker”: the object of evaluation is the burden a representation choice imposes on recipients even when the speaker satisfies the specified truth and correction conditions. It offers a bounded explanation of a distinct failure, rather than simply an instruction to store source names.

The information criterion is elementary factorization through an information partition. It supplies precision and a boundary to the norm, not mathematical originality. The whole contribution depends on the normative argument and the selective-loss diagnosis. A draft that merely gives the example and calls provenance important would fall below this passing assessment.

## Draft obligations and reopen conditions

- Cite and compare Frise explicitly. The point is not that equal present information with different past sources is a newly discovered problem.
- Distinguish the set of original report tokens affected by A's defect from the audience's total current entitlement. Once the mapping is lost, higher-order uncertainty may affect both beliefs. Calling B's original report intact is compatible with loss of present permission to treat a particular report as B-backed.
- Keep the guarantee conditional. Failure to identify the unaffected report does not prove that all rational agents must suspend both beliefs under every stake or probability distribution.
- Explain why the relay owes preservation rather than merely possessing useful information. Invited consequential reliance, foreseeable defeaters, cheap preservation, no overriding privacy duty, and avoidable recipient burden are the working grounds. They require defense, not just recitation.
- The current title can mislocate causation. Correction reveals the loss; the avoidable earlier erasure and the defeater jointly create the problem. Do not imply truthful disclosure itself wrongs the audience.
- Reopen novelty if Doyle's full text, Boyd, or another primary work already develops this same selective-loss responsibility argument. Reopen adequacy if the draft quietly equates source defeat, falsity, and categorical withdrawal.
