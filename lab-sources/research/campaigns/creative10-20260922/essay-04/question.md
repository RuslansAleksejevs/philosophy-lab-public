# Initial questions and alternatives

Author attempt 04; broad area language and communication. 2026-09-22. AI-originated public research rationale, before literature retrieval and before any manuscript.

1. **Can a public correction undo testimony without access to its audience's reasons?** A withdrawal should remove reliance on this speaker's claim, while preserving independently supported belief. Two listeners may now believe the same propositions but differ over which depend on the speaker. A content-only correction apparently cannot specify the appropriate result for both. Possible increment: a precise limitation on retraction as a public speech act, with consequences for what constitutes adequate correction rather than merely psychological correction success. Chosen for investigation, not yet endorsed.
2. **Can parties genuinely agree when they deliberately leave a term interpreted differently?** Investigate whether invariance of practical commitments across each permitted interpretation suffices for agreement, and whether future contingencies destroy that sufficiency. Deferred: likely close to incompletely theorized agreement and planning accounts of shared agency.
3. **Can a translation promise be fulfilled when its recipients use incompatible tests of fidelity?** Distinguish preserving a speaker's commitments from preserving audience inferences; ask when these are jointly impossible. Deferred: substantial overlap likely with translation indeterminacy and conceptual engineering, and too broad in current form.

The selected direction must survive searches on retraction, belief contraction and recovery, iterated belief revision, testimony defeat, truth-maintenance/provenance, and correction obligations. A mathematical-looking impossibility alone would not establish novelty. If it merely restates standard belief contraction's dependence on entrenchment, it must be revised or rejected before drafting. The intended philosophical result must concern the relation between a speech act's public object and its differentiated rational uptake.
