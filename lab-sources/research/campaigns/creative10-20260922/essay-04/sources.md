## Decision

Self-assessed PASS_RELATIVE applies narrowly to the collateral-loss counterexample plus correction-relevant distinction criterion and conditional earlier relay duty described in proposal.md. It is not a verdict that a theorem is mathematically new, that provenance responsibility is new, or that no scholar has made this argument. General removal-versus-retraction and provenance-tracking proposals are rejected as contributions. The independent critic should test especially whether Doyle's stronger unavailable full text already states the normative increment, and whether the paired-history result adds enough beyond a familiar duty of care.

The criterion assumes a fixed range of defeater notices, no new reconstructive evidence, and a guarantee of selective support withdrawal. Ordinary probabilistic belief and the permissibility of taking risks require separate discussion. The proposal is a bounded normative argument rather than a new theory of truth, meaning or inference.
