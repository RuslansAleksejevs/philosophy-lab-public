# Response to both independent reviews — essay 07

Decision: REVISE and retain the narrow candidate. The final keeps the approved thesis: valid prospective renewal can coexist with an unperformed accountability undertaking to former trial participants. No general incompatibility among democratic safeguards, absolute originality, new source of obligation, or permanent former-member franchise is claimed. The original draft and both reviews remain unchanged.

## Argument opponent

1. **Stronger assurance not earned by the example — ACCEPTED.** The workshop now explicitly undertakes to assess participants' complaints, give reasoned answers and implement warranted corrections, without a continued-membership condition. This is a stipulated substantive undertaking, not something inferred from a sunset date or the word democratic. A contrasting aggregate-only evaluation can satisfy its more limited terms. The final acknowledges that explicitness makes the duty straightforward under familiar fidelity principles; the remaining contribution is institutional diagnosis and a bounded repair.
2. **Composition rhetoric exceeds the argument — ACCEPTED.** Replaced the claim that the three safeguards mysteriously fail to perform their own functions with the exact invalid inference: their success does not establish performance of an additional accountability undertaking. Added both controls: refusal to hear complaints without exit also fails; exit with preserved complaints machinery does not fail. The mechanism is enduring claims plus membership-conditioned access. Selective exit explains systematic exposure of the most dissatisfied and the way valid renewal can obscure this gap.
3. **Jurisdictions have interacting consequences — ACCEPTED.** Renewal authority is explicitly bounded by warranted remedies. Added a harder example: restoring wrongfully withdrawn prepaid access may change the next year's schedule. Former members can hold such claims without a general future-policy vote. A duty to answer does not itself determine whether that remedy is warranted. Recommendations count only with a competent accountable decision-maker and an effective route to implementation; an ignorable recommendation is insufficient.
4. **Personal access is sometimes a means, not the end — ACCEPTED.** The central necessary condition now concerns effective consideration and response, not universal personal participation. Added an automatic independent auditor as an alternative. Distinguished new complaints, new evidence and appeals; indefinite access to all three is not established.

## Contribution/source critic

1. **Hirschman's contribution is more than selective depletion — ACCEPTED.** Final credits both the causal selection mechanism and the interaction of exit and voice. Removed novelty rhetoric about the mere combination of protections. Corrected the chapter-4 locator from pp. 51–55 to pp. 51–54. Source uptake is bounded to the critic's saved primary-text excerpts in a third-party OCR transcription.
2. **Retain narrow conditional scope of necessary institutional repair — ACCEPTED.** Necessity is stated for delivering the stronger assurance in the specified designs. No claim is made that every temporary collective policy needs the same complaint apparatus.
3. **Effective consequences of recommendations — ACCEPTED.** Addressed jointly with opponent point 3: identified competent disposition and implementation, with reasons and accountability. Binding remedies are not categorically demanded for every undertaking.
4. **Clarify undertaking to participants rather than only current members — ACCEPTED.** Addressed jointly with opponent point 1. The final does not infer the undertaking merely from initial consent.
5. **Avoid empirical generalization about why people consent — ACCEPTED / MAINTAINED.** The final retains modal, illustrative language. It does not claim measured evidence that review assurances generally cause consent.
6. **Preserve Erman/Molloy/Kuyper attribution and limits — ACCEPTED / MAINTAINED.** Function-sensitive inclusion is inherited; adequate sunset review is established; temporary escape need not remove membership. No criticism of those authors' entire programs is advanced. Additional Campos discussion is unnecessary because no broad boundary-priority claim remains.

No substantive criticism was rejected. These changes repair and narrow exposition rather than replace the approved thesis. No repeated general novelty search is represented as having occurred. The contribution remains modest: an institutional diagnosis and resolution built from familiar normative resources. Neither reviewers nor revision establish full literature coverage, universal priority, a theory of valid waiver, or transfer to state citizenship.
