## Defensible increment
The contribution is a failure of composition: valid initial authorization, exit protection and fair renewal can jointly fail the specific assurance of later answerability that helped justify the trial. Crucially, this defect survives full information, sincere voting and legitimate membership change. Preserving a separate past-facing claim avoids freezing the future electorate. This is a modest but substantive limit on a justification for provisional democratic arrangements.

## Required precision in drafting
1. Keep the defended review assurance explicit. Expiration alone promises no retrospective hearing.
2. Separate answerability from release, forgiveness and participant veto. Independent review may discharge the duty without each claimant's approval.
3. Explain the specifically democratic significance: the assurance helps make initial consent to an uncertain collective trial reasonable. Otherwise this risks being an ordinary contractual case.
4. Ask whether causal exit is necessary. Independently departing subjects may retain the same promise-based claim; causation may explain the systematic vulnerability without grounding standing.
5. Do not presume departure normally removes complaint rights. The objection targets arrangements that actually connect review standing to current membership.
6. Avoid saying past harms become reversible. Review can provide contestability or warranted redress.
7. Acknowledge Erman and Molloy as close precedents; neither needs refutation.
