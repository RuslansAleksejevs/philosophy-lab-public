## What is inherited and what may be new

Elementary contraposition, the distinction between a function and an injective function, and the general fact that evidence need not resemble its object are inherited tools. Repackaging these as a memory theory would not establish novelty. Nor is the claim that imperfect memories sometimes inform us distinctive.

The defensible increment is instead a targeted non-subtractive reply: accepting interdependence of phenomenal parts does not rule out a many-to-one transformation of whole phenomenal types that permits inference from output inequality to input inequality. The loss of distinctions simultaneously blocks an inference from output equality. This identifies two different logical requirements inside a specific dispute. The countermodel changes what must be defended without pretending to satisfy that requirement in actual recollection. That is modest but substantive enough for a draft. My independent searches found no prior work presenting this particular reply with its directional evidential distinction; absence from these searches is not proof of priority.

## Conditions the manuscript must preserve

1. The finite mapping is a consistency model relative to the stated holism premise. Abstract symbols alone do not establish that a psychologically possible human memory process realizes it.
2. Restrict the direct result to two outputs of the same appropriately matched procedure. Comparing present perception with a recollection invokes different transformations and does not inherit the inference automatically.
3. The central empirical assumption is that mapping from phenomenal types to outputs is well-defined across the compared episodes. A deterministic mapping from complete physical histories would not suffice: distinct nonphenomenal histories can produce distinct memories of phenomenally identical experiences.
4. Keep deterministic guarantees distinct from probabilistic support. Equal output distributions do not imply equal realized outputs. A noisy version needs an error model; it cannot simply reuse the contraposition.
5. Say that equal outputs do not entail equal inputs under the weak condition. They may still supply probabilistic evidence under additional assumptions. Calling all null contrast evidence worthless would overstate the result.
6. Do not conclude that a high-level perceptual content has been located. Some past phenomenal difference, its perceptual location, and its intentional explanation remain separate claims.

These are execution constraints already compatible with the proposal, not requirements for a different thesis.
