# Can distorted recollection still establish phenomenal difference?

AI research rationale, 2026-09-22. No manuscript yet.

Three initial directions were considered. (1) Attention and the act of introspection may change the experience being compared: rejected as an initial contribution because existing phenomenal-contrast criticism already covers nonphenomenal and attentional rivals. (2) A trial of a transformative experience can destroy the original option of choosing it without advance acquaintance: set aside because the initial search located the established revelation/transformative-decision debate and no sufficiently specific increment was yet defended. This is not a claim that every possible development of that idea is old. (3) Irreversible learning makes a novice/expert phenomenal comparison depend on memory: the generic objection was rejected after finding Nourbakhshi (2023), which directly challenges recollection-based contrasts.

Selected development of (3): answer the strong memory objection, rather than repeat it. Nourbakhshi explicitly considers partial similarity and rejects a subtraction of memory-mode phenomenology using holism. A different route compares two recollections under a stable transformation of whole experiences. Holistic change does not itself entail creation of spurious differences. A function can lose information and radically change every phenomenal part while retaining the implication that unequal transformed outputs have unequal inputs. This licenses difference under restricted evidential conditions, but never equality without additional injectivity assumptions, and never locates the difference in the contested high-level content.

Proposed contribution: an explicit countermodel to the necessity of full phenomenal reconstruction that accepts holistic transformation, plus a principled asymmetry between positive and null contrast evidence. The specific difficulty is that actual recollections may depend on episode-specific beliefs, elapsed time and attention as well as original phenomenology; same psychological mechanism is insufficient. That difficulty must become a stated identification condition, not be hidden behind the mathematical example.

Search records and exact accessed web outputs will accompany the selected proposal. Relative novelty remains subject to an independent pre-draft gate; no absolute priority is claimed.
