# Creative independent essays

Create a worthwhile original philosophical contribution, written clearly in English.
Russian is used for short reader summaries and process explanations. English is a
coordinator default for the manuscript, not an explicit user language choice.
Requested model/effort: gpt-6-astra / medium at all author, critic and revision stages.
No minimum length or target length; develop the actual argument adequately within
6000 words excluding bibliography. Do not stretch a small idea into an essay.

Choose several genuinely different candidate ideas within your broad assigned area.
Save a concise public research rationale and rejected alternatives, not private
chain of thought. Prefer meaningful novelty to exotic terminology. You may cross
disciplinary boundaries when useful. Do not force an AI topic or a policy checklist.
All questions, theses, examples, arguments and revisions originate from AI.

Before writing a manuscript, use actual internet searches to look for the closest
existing arguments. Search conceptual paraphrases, adjacent vocabulary, specialist
literature and citations from relevant work. Open and read the closest primary works
as access permits. Search snippets alone cannot certify a difference. Preserve
queries, dates, result URLs, bibliographic details, exact accessed excerpts or web
tool outputs, locators, actual full/excerpt/abstract-only coverage, and inaccessible
works. Respect quote and summary limits; avoid large quotations in the manuscript.
For each close work, compare its thesis, argument and conclusion to your proposed
increment. A new label, scenario or application alone does not establish a worthwhile
philosophical contribution. If the main argument already exists, reject or change
the proposal, preserve the rejected version, and search again BEFORE drafting.
State PASS_RELATIVE only with a defensible specific increment; never claim proven
absolute originality. If the best candidate remains uncertain, investigate further
or choose another. Do not hide adverse novelty evidence to fill the ten slots.

Write a complete essay with a precise thesis, defended premises, serious objections,
replies, concrete examples, fair engagement with the closest literature, limitations
and bibliography with usable links. A coherent modest result is preferable to an
unsupported sweeping claim. Distinguish interpretation from established source facts.

Two critics work in fresh sessions: an argument opponent and a contribution/source
critic. They each see only your own sealed draft and sources, not each other's reviews.
After both are archived, revise and answer each substantive criticism with accepted,
partially accepted or rejected and reasons. Preserve all versions. If the thesis
changes substantially, repeat the novelty check before revising. A failed thesis can
be set aside honestly; never call an unresolved failure a successful contribution.

Only read this common neutral packet, your task and your own essay folder. Shared
filesystem access does not authorize looking at other essays. Separate contexts
are a process fact, not proof of intellectual independence.
