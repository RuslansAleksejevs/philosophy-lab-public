## Strongest unresolved objection and concrete repair

The alleged factual mistake principally concerns Cal. Since Ada correctly admits her own negligence, a defender can say her apology for that negligence is already complete; Bea simply conditions her public response on clearing up a separate person's role. The weather analogy is answered, but the original invitation analogy could still leave this narrower separation available.

Add a short explicit explanation of the argumentative standard. The case need not show that every reasonable recipient must withhold acceptance, or that every accurate apology must inventory all other perpetrators. It needs to establish that Ada has voluntarily made an exclusive attribution constitutive of this particular offered acknowledgment, so declining the package is a genuine available response. The current draft gestures at this; make the distinction between voluntary inclusion and a universal completeness requirement unmistakable.

Also sharpen the hesitation variant: Bea may be unsure about Cal while already knowing everything needed to judge Ada's unchanged negligence. Otherwise the new uncertainty could make the stipulation of fixed forgiveness look artificial or unstable. This is clarification of the approved case, not a request to add a different one.
