## Charitable reconstruction

The essay does not establish a general duty of acceptance. Its argument is conditional: if a particular interpersonal consideration grounds a local response, then the mere existence of unsettled claims elsewhere does not defeat that consideration. Universal content of reform does not itself give an apology universal addressees. Specific reasons can nevertheless connect claims: misleading endorsement, unfair resource allocation, evidence of insincerity, practical harm, or collective ownership. Separating powers, duties and consequences is a strength. The defense of victims who wish to accept without speaking for other victims is also valuable.

## 1. Major: the controlled comparison varies the wrong feature

Location: section 3, from the initial community claim through the added second owner; sections 5–6.

The public claim is expressly unsettled in both cases. The comparison therefore establishes, at most, that an additional independent victim need not further weaken a stipulated local reason. It cannot show that the already-present public incompleteness did not weaken that reason, or the overall justification for requiring the response, in both cases. A critic could accept every verdict in the pair while saying that public incompleteness already removed one important justification before the comparison began. An unchanged residual reason would not refute that claim.

The essay partly concedes this in section 5, but still lets the comparison carry the broader conclusion about public incompleteness. Distinguish (a) increasing the number of unsettled claims, (b) introducing public incompleteness, and (c) correcting an exaggerated estimate of public benefit. These are different changes.

Repair: state exactly which inference the two-owner pair disproves. If a claim about public incompleteness remains central, consider a separate pair in which public repair is complete in one case and incomplete in the other while the proposed interpersonal basis is independently specified. Explain why the difference is morally irrelevant to that particular basis. This need not prove an actual general acceptance duty, but it must do more than stipulate its immunity.

## 2. Major: the charitable interpretation threatens the contribution

Location: section 1, second paragraph; section 5, first two paragraphs.

Hay explicitly allows local adjustment at lines 176–180 and separates local from social procedures at 203–206. His public-benefit argument at 188–198 describes comprehensive recalibration as the supposed source of the obligation. These passages support the draft's charitable interpretation: he challenges an alleged justification, rather than directly asserting that every independent interpersonal reason becomes weaker. His note 8 also reserves the individual victim's own decision. [Hay, original article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41296-024-00743-8).

The issue is therefore whether the essay has identified a real transfer between justificatory grounds, or merely a possible overreading it correctly rejects. The former could be a worthwhile correction; the latter is a useful clarification with a smaller dialectical result. The draft currently acknowledges the weaker interpretation, then attacks the stronger inference without establishing that the text relies on it.

Repair: identify the exact contested premise and explain why it misdescribes the interpersonal argument under discussion. Do not simply say that local duties could have independent grounds: show why the relevant proposed ground is independent of comprehensive public success. Alternatively, openly frame the result as a limit on what the social objection establishes, with no claim to have exposed Hay's own invalid inference. That repair preserves the distinction but may reduce the distinctive contribution substantially. This risk should be faced rather than hidden in qualifications.

## 3. Repairable: acknowledgement is thinner than the duty under dispute

Location: section 3, opening and final paragraphs; section 5, second paragraph.

There is an important difference between ceasing to describe someone falsely as refusing acknowledgement, communicating one's belief in sincerity, and ending negative partiality grounded in the original wrong. The last paragraph of section 3 tests only the first. Someone can stop making a false attribution while declining communicative uptake, and can sincerely acknowledge remorse while continuing to give the wrongdoer's interests less weight. Thus showing one narrow response survives unsettled claims does not show that the disputed package survives them.

The essay need not defend the entire package. But it should specify the exact component that its counterexample protects. A constructive repair is to give the owner's actual response and the exact further treatment it permits or requires. Explicitly leave the stronger behavioral requirement unresolved if it is not established. Otherwise the discussion moves from a relatively uncontroversial demand for accuracy to a controversial reparative obligation without argument.

## 4. Repairable: invariant reason, surviving duty and overall justification are not interchangeable

Location: section 3, paragraph beginning “The comparison exposes”; section 6, central conclusion.

Suppose a response has an interpersonal reason in its favor and an additional public benefit. Learning that the benefit will not occur can weaken the total justification even when the interpersonal reason retains precisely its weight. That is a coherent position, not necessarily a scope error. Conversely, survival of a consideration does not by itself show that a pro tanto duty remains: that depends on the account of what makes a consideration obligation-generating.

Repair: keep separate the identity/weight of a particular reason, the presence of an obligation grounded in it, and the all-things-considered justification. Section 5 makes much of this distinction already; use the same language consistently elsewhere. The safe conclusion is a burden of explanation for claims that public incompleteness undermines this specified interpersonal basis, not an assurance that the whole case for obligation remains equally strong.

## 5. Repairable: the waiver argument's best objection concerns assurance, not permission to harm

Location: section 2, paragraphs beginning “Now suppose” and “The broad content”.

The essay assumes the owner's power to waive her own apologetic address. That is legitimate for rejecting an inference from universal content alone, but not as a demonstration that such a power exists in every relevant case. More importantly, the strongest objection need not say that waiver cancels the underlying duty of care. It could concern an additional duty to make one's recognition and reform publicly answerable. First-order care can remain intact while a duty of assurance remains unperformed.

Repair: distinguish the duty of care, the private address, and any independent duty of assurance. Then say exactly which is waived and why that waiver does not purport to discharge the others. The garden example already points in this direction. The point about not authorizing harm is correct, but does not by itself answer the stronger assurance objection.

## What should not be abandoned

Do not respond by making the counterexample prove that solidarity never matters, or by adding an unsupported general duty for victims. Idealization is legitimate for testing an entailment, and the draft responsibly identifies several ways real cases violate its assumptions. The distinction between a beneficiary and a represented claimant is clear. The different treatment of waiver and acknowledgment should remain.

The priority is to tighten the target and the test, not multiply examples. A successful revision would identify one exact scope transition, show why its relevant premises fail to support it, and describe the limited normative result without sliding between reasons, duties and total justification.

Uncertainties: I have not independently verified all of Fabre's relevant passages or all the other cited authors. Whether the sharper correction is already present elsewhere remains open. If the strongest charitable reconstruction of the target already accepts the essay's full limited conclusion, set aside the claim to refutation and reassess whether the remaining clarification is substantial enough.
