## Target fidelity
Hay does not deny all local authority: his text expressly grants local settlement (web-2, article lines176–177), and his final two-phase procedure preserves voluntary bilateral acceptance before community uptake (web-4, lines203–212). The strongest charitable reading is that missing public benefits weaken a purported societal justification, while victim burdens independently defeat obligation. On that reading he is right about incomplete public repair. Nevertheless, lines176 and197–198 invite the stronger conclusions about inability to waive and diminished obligation that the proposal targets. The correction must be framed as a challenge to those inferential extensions, not discovery of bilateral repair or refutation of his entire position. [Hay](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41296-024-00743-8).

## Nearest overlap
Bennett already gives the relevant normative architecture: victims can waive directed obligations while other obligations survive; acceptance, discharge, waiver and forgiveness differ. The two-bicycle case by itself is thus an illustration of existing theory. [Bennett 2018](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/133937/1/Forgiveness%20as%20Normative%20Power%20PAPA%20final.pdf), web-2, manuscript pp11–14.
My independent search also found Bennett's recent historical-injustice paper: apology need not fit a precisely bilateral model and can accomplish normative work despite imperfectly identified recipients. This blocks any broad claim to discover useful but incomplete or non-bilateral apology; the accessed sections do not supply the particular correction proposed here. [Bennett, The Relevance of Apology to Reparations for Historical Injustice](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/japp.70098), web-3 and web-4.

Fabre provides direct interpersonal grounds and rejects community readmission as her model. However, manuscript p17 expressly distinguishes recognizing an already changed relation in acceptance from exercising a normative power in forgiveness. Use the waiver argument and the acceptance argument separately, with the latter conditional on unchanged grounds for acknowledgment. ## Concrete increment and significance
The defensible increment is a bounded negative result about a later objection: universal prospective content does not establish universal authority of the addressee; incomplete total repair does not itself defeat an independently grounded local demand. The controlled comparison tests precisely which extra premise makes another claimant's presence relevant. It allows the social criticism to succeed when actual linkage exists, rather than dismissing social repair. This is more than relabeling if the manuscript explicitly reconstructs both disputed inferences and states what survives under the charitable reading above.

The important result is conditional. The essay cannot infer a duty from mere power, from the intuitive acceptability of the first owner's choice, or from the absence of one defeater. Nor may its countermodel exclude collective harm merely by defining the problem away: grant that a public claim remains, then explain why its remaining unsettled need not change the specified local reasons. Counterexamples with coupled claims establish limits, not failure of every independent local settlement. These are sharpening requirements within the exact approved thesis, not authorization to replace it.
