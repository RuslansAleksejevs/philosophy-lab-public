# Essay 13: public question and candidate selection

2026-09-22. AI-originated proposal; pre-draft stage only.

Broad question: what must be added to causal continuity to obtain an individual's personal temporal order?

Three candidates considered:

1. A duplication-neutral measure of concern for future persons. Initial thought: branch refinement should not itself multiply prudential reasons. Rejected as a starting contribution: the search immediately located Greaves's caring-measure account of Everettian branching, and this initial thought supplies no adequately defended increment. No full Greaves reading or refutation claimed.
2. Whether fission splits inherited promises or duplicates them. Initial thought: distinguish identity inheritance from creditors' claims. Set aside as undeveloped: early combined searches supplied no dependable primary coverage of this question, and a debt-division application alone is not a contribution. No novelty verdict for the literature claimed.
3. A precise correction to the claim that causal dependence, unlike a metric, suffices to give personal temporal order. Selected for independent review. Maslen (2024), section 4, explicitly endorses causal ordering while acknowledging missing duration. A familiar self-encounter creates a directed cycle among stages of one individual. Restricting causation to persistence links is a familiar response, already present in Hall/Wasserman. The proposed increment is deliberately narrower: separate three required results—choice of persistence edges, existence of an order, and determination of an order—and show why the multiple-instance artwork example offered in the same article fails the last even after a familiar edge restriction. A single scalar personal history requires a further linear-history assumption; mere persistence is insufficient.

Selection is provisional pending the independent novelty check. The elementary graph facts and immanent/transeunt distinction are NOT claimed as discoveries. If the critic finds the article already contains the relevant qualification or the targeted correction adds too little, set aside rather than draft a renamed familiar view.
