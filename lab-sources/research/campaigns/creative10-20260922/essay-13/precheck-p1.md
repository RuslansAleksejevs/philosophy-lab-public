## Defensible increment

The worthwhile result is a target-specific limit on extending causal personal time from individual careers to multiply instantiated works. A selected persistence relation can determine provenance without determining every comparison between realization events. The artwork motivation makes this more than replacing a person's fission with a sculpture: multiple instantiation is accepted within the target's own domain, so rejecting personal fission does not remove the problem. A branching genealogy can retain sanctioned identity and still lack a unique total chronology.

The underlying graph results are elementary. The cycle example and restriction to identity-making causation are inherited. Neither qualifies as this proposal's discovery. What survives is the separation of selecting constitutive edges from obtaining a determinate total order in a case expressly used to motivate generality. That is sufficient for a modest philosophical correction, provided the draft keeps this scale.

## Charitable target reading

Maslen's section 4 directly considers multiple instances, artist authorization and a mold sent into the past, then offers a causal ordering of the previously considered cases. The text also quotes Wasserman's restricted identity-making relation. The strongest charitable interpretation therefore already excludes arbitrary self-directed causal influence. The young/old feedback example refutes an unrestricted reading only; it must not be sold as refuting her considered view.

A second charitable interpretation understands ordering as partial, or understands the selected individual as a single career. On either interpretation there is no inconsistency. But the first does not yet supply a complete scalar personal chronology, and the second requires saying which career carries the artwork's personal time. The proposal identifies this missing choice rather than proving that all causal definitions fail. Maslen's main aim is transmigration, not a comprehensive ontology of artwork time; characterize this as a qualification of the generalization, not demolition of her central project. ## Draft obligations and failure triggers

1. State the object of the graph exactly: realization events or stage tokens, not automatically temporal parts of an artwork type. Explain why the target's sanctioned-instance example licenses this representation. Numerical identity of work, distinctness of casts and a persistence/provenance relation are different claims.
2. Address aggregate artwork stages seriously. An externally dated aggregate has an order, but the burden is explaining how that order also tracks the backward provenance invoked by the mold example. Do not assume that every possible aggregate construction fails.
3. Distinguish lack of a unique total chronology from failure to detect any backward trip. A comparable source–arrival pair can already witness a reversal in external order, even if other branches are incomparable. The proposal's two possible linear extensions need not change whether time travel occurs somewhere.
4. For the finite graph theorem distinguish a coordinate assignment increasing on edges, an injective total ranking, and a faithful representation of the causal partial order. Acyclicity allows increasing assignments; incomparable vertices can receive either relative ranking (or equal values if ties are permitted). Thus a unique scalar chronology is not forced. Do not infer that no scalar assignment exists merely from branching.
5. Keep the contribution conditional on the generalization actually requiring a complete individual chronology. If the best defended reading requires only a partial order sufficient for selected backward-travel verdicts, narrow the conclusion accordingly. If the artwork ontology already excludes the proposed branching provenance, the central counterexample has not been established.

These are development obligations within the sealed thesis, not a demand for a new research direction. A draft that centers the familiar self-loop instead, or asserts general failure of backward-travel detection, would not realize this PASS.
