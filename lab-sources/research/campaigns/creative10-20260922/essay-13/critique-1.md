# Independent argument review: essay 13

Recommendation: **REVISE**, retaining the conditional result but strengthening its philosophical consequence. No fatal defect in the finite-order argument was found. The largest unresolved issue is whether its extra demand is needed by the target account at all.

## Charitable reconstruction

The essay grants that appropriately authorized production may preserve the identity of a multiply instantiated artwork. It also grants the familiar restriction from arbitrary causation to relevant constitutive causation. A common source can nevertheless lead to two independently produced realizations without either descending from the other. Their provenance therefore fixes some personal comparisons but does not entail a strict total order of all realization events. This is distinct from the inability to measure durations. Partial orders and lineage-relative histories remain available, and the essay correctly concedes that the backward route is identifiable on either account.

That is a valid insufficiency argument about what follows from the stipulated relation. It does not establish the impossibility of an artwork chronology, the indeterminacy of every temporal fact, or the failure of a causal diagnosis of backward travel. The draft generally respects those limits.

## Major repairable defects

### 1. The desired total chronology is still an additional demand

Locations: opening thesis; “Two descendants of one work,” final two paragraphs; “A partial history may be enough.”

The strongest opponent accepts every arrow and responds: my causal account provides exactly the comparisons needed to identify the journey. It never promised to order independent realizations. The draft already acknowledges this response, but does not show what explanatory task remains unsatisfied once it is accepted. Both total extensions agree that S precedes B personally, while B precedes S externally. Thus no actual travel verdict in the example changes. The uncertainty about A versus B could be harmless surplus structure that an adequate theory should decline to provide.

This matters because the essay's interesting application risks collapsing into a conditional whose antecedent nobody needs. The provided Maslen passage motivates a general order, but generality of applicability is not itself totality of each resulting order. I do not independently establish her intended interpretation here; the stronger interpretive attribution remains unproven.

**Repair:** explicitly distinguish a criticism of total-coordinate completeness from a criticism of the backward-travel criterion. State what further question a single work-wide chronology would answer and why that question deserves an answer. One concrete consequence worth developing is that an arbitrary linear extension must not count a reversed ordering between causally incomparable realizations as evidence of another journey. Define the diagnosis using a comparable provenance pair, then explain why this constraint is necessary when moving from genealogy to coordinates. This would give the clarification a practical conceptual consequence without pretending the existing S-to-B verdict fails. Alternatively, defend the narrower essay as a scope clarification and remove suggestions that Maslen's achieved explanatory purpose has been undermined.

### 2. Realization events have not yet earned all the commitments of personal stages

Locations: “Two descendants of one work,” especially “That representation earns its relevance...” and the following paragraph.

The argument appropriately avoids equating two bronze objects. But the assertion that realization events belong somewhere in a work's history does not entail that they must occupy separate positions in a single personal time, or that their productive relation must be the relation of persistence between stages of that bearer. S is the preparation of a source, rather than plainly a realization of the work. Authorizing and reproducing a work can be necessary for a cast to count as its instance without functioning as personal succession of the work itself.

The proposed dilemma is consequently too quick: rejecting production as persistence need not reject the ordinary claim that a work has been carried into the past. A proponent can make the travel claim derivative from the backward transport of an authorized carrier, with no personal time assigned to the abstract or multiply realized whole. The draft discusses a two-level answer, but treats the original work-level extension as though it already required the stronger bridge.

**Repair:** display the bridge as a separate assumption: if a work travels in the relevant robust sense, every authorized transmission that carries its identity supplies a personally increasing relation among specified events. Explain why the target should accept it, or make the result expressly conditional on it. Clarify whether S represents an existing realization, its transmission, or an event involving an already existing work. Give the derivative-travel response its strongest formulation. The remaining contribution then concerns an account that demands work-level personal succession, rather than all reasonable descriptions of an artwork arriving in the past.

### 3. Different senses of determination need separating

Locations: “What the arrows establish”; “A partial history may be enough,” first paragraph; title.

The mathematical result concerns entailment by the selected relational data. It does not show that the world lacks additional temporal facts, that there is no privileged extension given the complete history, or that a question has no answer until a convention is chosen. The draft sometimes moves from “the arrows do not determine” to the stronger “has no answer.” A theory may accept provenance as evidence for some relations while taking further temporal relations as primitive. That would defeat causal sufficiency, but not determinate personal time.

**Repair:** distinguish (i) underdetermination by provenance, (ii) a deliberate partial-order theory in which incomparable pairs have no personal before/after relation, and (iii) a richer theory with additional facts. Mark the no-answer language as a commitment of option (ii). The conditional central theorem needs only (i), which is also its strongest defensible conclusion.

## Mathematical and model checks

The finite directed-graph claims are sound under the specified assumptions: acyclicity permits an increasing injective ranking, and incomparability permits opposite orders in different linear extensions. The discussion rightly separates preservation of arrows from reflection of the entire relation. It should continue to avoid claiming that every assignment of scalar coordinates must be injective. For the isolated fork S-to-A and S-to-B, equal coordinates for A and B are possible. That does not establish actual simultaneity; it shows that insisting on a strict total ranking is one additional choice. The more general X-before-Y with Z incomparable to both correctly explains why representing every incomparable pair as equality cannot work for arbitrary partial orders.

An omission of causal links in an actual case would weaken the conclusion, but the fictional independent branches address that objection by stipulation. Independence of production should remain explicitly about the selected constitutive relation, not the absence of all causal interaction. The draft already makes that distinction and should preserve it.

## Optional improvements and preservation of the contribution

Shorten the self-teaching cycle discussion. It is useful background, but neither its familiar objection nor the standard immanent-causation repair does the principal work after the restriction is granted. Spend the recovered space on the work-level bridge and the consequences of using arbitrary linear extensions in travel judgments.

Do not try to rescue significance by claiming a novel graph theorem, refutation of all causal personal time, or general impossibility of artworks traveling. Those would erase the draft's chief merit: isolating a precise insufficiency while preserving the successful local comparison. The proposed repairs preserve that result; they may reduce its status from an objection to a published theory to a carefully justified clarification of its possible outputs.
