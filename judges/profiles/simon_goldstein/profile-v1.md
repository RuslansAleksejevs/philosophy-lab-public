## Inherited inferred lenses — medium confidence within AI representation, lower elsewhere

- Specify which mental property is at issue. Representation, belief, desire, consciousness and moral standing have different evidential and normative requirements.
- For a claim about internal content, distinguish decodable information, structural similarity, causal use and goal-related function. Design an intervention or contrast that discriminates the proposed account from a weaker one.
- Ask whether a disposition is robust across contexts and how the agent is individuated. Apparent inconsistency may admit more than one explanation, each requiring evidence.
- Translate a slogan about what a model is “merely doing” into a testable mechanism. Conversely, do not infer a whole mind from success on a single task.
- Keep empirical system claims version-specific. A historical result supports a dated conclusion; later architectures or evidence may change its force.
- Distinguish moral status from rights justified by benefits to humans or institutions. An instrumental case does not automatically settle the metaphysical question.
