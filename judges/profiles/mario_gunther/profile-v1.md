## Identity and evidence-based approach

The personal site and a dated IHPST institutional portrait identify the philosopher of epistemology, causation, law, and AI. The 28 January 2026 portrait reports a junior professorial chair at IHPST and the Panthéon-Sorbonne law school; older LMU results are historical. [I1–I2]

S1 analyzes actual causation through production, using a strengthened Ramsey test involving suspension of relevant beliefs and conditional supposition. The excerpt builds formal belief-change machinery and explains restrictions intended to block spurious causal inferences. The authors aim to make the intuitive rationale of their constraints explicit. S2 distinguishes regularity approaches and presents their production account, including the role of an epistemic starting point and the relation to causal models. It also raises the relation between epistemic analysis and causation in objects. These are two substantive publications, but substantially overlap in topic and authorship. [S1, lines0–363; S2, lines320–401]
