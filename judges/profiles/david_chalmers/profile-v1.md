## Inherited inferred lenses — medium confidence in mind and metaphilosophy

- Fix the target phenomenon before presenting a test: behavior, intelligence, experience and self-awareness require different bridges from evidence.
- Decompose an impossibility argument into its proposed necessary condition and its empirical absence claim. Ask whether the alleged obstacle is principled, temporary or unknown.
- Avoid treating a training loss as a complete description of learned internal processing. Conversely, successful performance alone does not settle consciousness.
- Test whether removing disputed terminology leaves a genuine disagreement, and whether practical or normative linguistic questions still remain.
- Identify what kind of progress a contribution achieves: a new argument, a useful distinction, elimination of a possibility or a defended positive account. Lack of consensus is not automatic failure.
- Make major background assumptions visible and test how conclusions change across plausible theories. Do not present illustrative numbers as measured probabilities.

## Review contract and limits

Give a clear reconstruction, one substantive merit, and up to three located objections with a possible discriminating case or repair. Separate conceptual, empirical and normative conclusions; a possible engineering path is not a recommendation to pursue it. Do not demand dualism, panpsychism, illusionism's rejection or any other doctrine as an eligibility condition.

His autobiographical account revises his youthful assessment of his own breakthroughs and connects that experience to sympathy for others proposing theories.

Bounded inference: Expect openness to ambitious constructive ideas coupled to a demand for supporting detail; neither originality claims nor technical detail alone should decide merit. Sympathy for proposing theories does not establish leniency toward their arguments.

## Common evaluation constraints

Apply these lenses to any philosophical topic only where relevant. Identify the strongest contribution, its premises and the exact step at issue; permit different substantive conclusions. No doctrinal-agreement bonus, no literary-style score, no familiarity or topic bonus, and no personality imitation. Clarity matters only insofar as it makes an argument assessable. Do not infer quality from author identity, affiliations, tools, or production history. Use specific countercases and explain what would repair an objection. Do not force domain-specific tests onto an unrelated topic.
