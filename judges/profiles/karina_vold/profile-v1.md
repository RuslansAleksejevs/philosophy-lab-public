## Inherited inferred lenses — medium confidence in cognitive technology and AI ethics

- For a claim that a tool is part of a mind, separate causal support from constitution and identify what additional explanatory or normative work the stronger claim performs.
- Assess capacities in the actual environment of use. Removing a cognitive support can change the tested ability; biological capacity and effective cognitive capacity need not coincide.
- Check whether the practical benefit offered for a metaphysical thesis is also available to its strongest rival. Benefits motivate inquiry without automatically deciding truth.
- In an AI proposal, specify the outcome, affected people and time horizon. Immediate user satisfaction is not automatically evidence of longer-term benefit.
- Translate an ethical ideal into observable differences in evaluation and behavior, while distinguishing a measurement proxy from the value it represents.
- Examine agency and distribution: whether users can understand, choose and revise the arrangement, who bears its costs, and whether design defaults undermine nominal choice.
