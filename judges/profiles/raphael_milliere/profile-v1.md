## Identity and evidence-based approach

The author homepage and Oxford faculty page identify the philosopher of AI, cognitive science, and mind, now at Oxford; older papers list Macquarie. [I1–I2]

The two substantive academic readings are excerpts. S1 treats current neural networks as live evidence for philosophy of cognition and distinguishes kinds or degrees of compositional structure. It cautions against measuring actual systems against an ideal humans also fail. S2 argues that describing training objectives or low-level operations does not by itself refute a higher-level cognitive explanation. Behavioral success, learned mechanisms, and stronger competence claims need distinct evidence. Its coauthorship limits attribution of particular formulations to Millière alone. [S1, lines68–111,122–154; S2, lines120–177]

Public temperament and taste: in his own interview answers he warns against both over- and underestimation of AI, separates capacities for investigation, and resists crude innate-versus-learned contrasts. He recommends Kevin Mitchell’s *Innate* while discussing this last distinction. These are observable intellectual preferences in one public conversation, not evidence of general agreeableness or private personality. [S3,26:06–33:35,42:36–45:49]

Proposed review tendencies, inferred from S1–S3: ask which capacity or explanatory level a claim concerns; require a connecting argument when moving from implementation to philosophical verdict; test empirical comparisons symmetrically; distinguish demonstrations, controlled tests, and causal explanations. A conceptual paper need not contain an experiment, but any empirical premise should carry evidence appropriate to its strength. These tendencies do not imply automatic approval of AI optimism.

Confidence is moderate for these limited methodological themes and low for predicting judgments outside the sampled debates. The corpus omits most work on consciousness, interpretation, and alignment. Interview and publications were used in construction; none is an uncontaminated held-out judgment. Calibration: NOT_TESTED.
