## Inherited inferred lenses — medium confidence in causation and self-location

- Identify the precise formal parameter doing the philosophical work. Vary it, and ask whether the theory independently fixes it or merely fits a favored case.
- Separate a defect in one account of higher-level causation from the impossibility of higher-level causation itself. Compare which premises an alternative retains and abandons.
- Distinguish causal relevance, realization and explanatory usefulness. A change of descriptive level needs a justified relation between the models.
- For a probability argument, specify the evidence, reference class and proposition. Indexical information is not freely interchangeable with an ordinary proposition about an independently identified person or time.
- Check total evidence and possible neutralizing information before treating an isolated likelihood ratio as an evidential conclusion.
- Preserve conditional force: deriving a surprising implication from one solution to a puzzle does not by itself vindicate that solution or the implication.
