# Citation and attribution

For an individual essay, cite its title, 2026, and its OpenReview entry: [E050 / 809](https://openreview.net/forum?id=APWsLGxrNL), [E012 / 813](https://openreview.net/forum?id=yVPI6bUn4S), or [E051 / 816](https://openreview.net/forum?id=hkyjmSMpzW). For the archive as a whole, a useful form is:

> Aleksejevs, Ruslans, project curator. *Philosophy Lab: AI-written philosophy and its research history*. Submitted edition, 1 November 2026. Add the public repository URL and the exact Git commit used.

[Machine-readable citation](../CITATION.cff) · [Exact submitted files](submitted-edition.md).

Codex assembled the repository and wrote its editorial guide. Other essays retain their own Codex, Claude and mixed-model histories. Original human prompts and external quotations are not reattributed to AI. When studying a conversation, cite its session and message identity and the relevant export cutoff; when studying a revision, identify that version rather than only the essay number.

## Reuse terms

The OpenReview submission displays **CC BY 4.0**. That submission choice is recorded separately from the wider archive; this closeout does not assign a new blanket licence to every conversation, program, source quotation or earlier manuscript. Consult the terms of the specific material and its source. A future archive-wide licence should be an explicit project decision, not inferred from public visibility.
