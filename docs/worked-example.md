# One argument, one repair: E012

A reader should be able to see what criticism changed. E012 provides a concrete example: the principle survived, but the case supporting it had to become more precise. This is a reconstruction of recorded work, not a new review.

## 1. The earlier claim

A fund promised to preserve photographs, lost them, and paid compensation. A later dispute then put the claimant in difficulty. The essay suggested that reasonable assistance might remain due even after the first payment.

[Exact manuscript supplied to the editor](../evidence/final-selection/updates/20261031/preselection/roles/E012/author/input/manuscript.md) · [Preserved original](../essays/E012/original.md)

## 2. The objection that mattered

In the difficult example, an institution wanted proof of what an earlier decision said. A photograph of a cracked wall would not establish the contents of that decision. The text had invoked the usefulness of the lost photographs without showing that they would help with its own example. That exposed a gap in the application, not a refutation of every possible continuing duty.

[Assigned problem](../evidence/final-selection/updates/20261031/preselection/roles/E012/author/input/TASK_RU.txt) · [Editor's explanation](../evidence/final-selection/updates/20261031/preselection/roles/E012/author/output/REPORT_RU.txt)

## 3. What the revision changed

The editor separated two cases. In the first, the later record falsely labels the damage as invented. A photograph could show that a crack existed, making a defence cheaper, even though it would not prove who caused it. In the second, only the contents of the earlier decision are disputed; photographs do not help, so their loss supplies no separate reason to pay for that process.

The revision also made the fund's promise stronger and the conclusion narrower: it had expressly undertaken to keep accessible, certified photographs for five years, including related later official disputes. The conclusion therefore does not follow from every duty to keep records. The cases and their administrative rules are explicitly hypothetical.

## 4. What the separate reviewer accepted

A fresh reviewer read the full revision and recommended keeping it for comparison. The paired cases now showed both a reason for assistance and a case where that reason disappears. The review retained two important questions: how much the narrow result contributes, and how to decide whether missing evidence would really have helped when its contents are uncertain.
