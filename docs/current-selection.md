## E050 · What can holding a system steady tell us about letting go?

Two systems agree on permanent holding outcomes but diverge after release. Within the specified family, a staged release converges to the intended equilibrium. The result depends on the stated family, preparation and time scale; it does not promise a common finite settling time across all parameter values. The calculations are in Appendix A of the same PDF.

## E012 · Can compensation leave an obligation unfinished?

A fund destroys photographs it promised to keep and compensates the loss. Later, another body falsely treats the claim as fabricated. If the photographs would have supplied an inexpensive defence, the first fund may still owe reasonable help. The contrasting case shows when the missing photographs would make no difference.

## E051 · Can memory reveal a difference without recreating an experience?

A comparison may preserve a relation between experiences without recreating either one. In the essay's deterministic model, a comparison can lose information yet still reveal a difference; matching outputs need an additional condition to establish sameness. The final version also distinguishes deterministic models from noisy procedures. These results do not establish that actual human memory meets the required conditions.
