# Responses to the two assigned reviews

This response is written before the revised manuscript. The revision is one editorial stage, not a new selection or review cycle. I have read the complete assigned draft and both reviews, verified all 53 assigned artifact hashes, read the source registers and access limits, and reconsulted the original published Fine–Williamson passages. The section numbers below specify the manuscript to be written next. Nothing here presumes that revision improves philosophical quality.

## Review A

### M1: Standalone neutrality versus undercutting earlier support — accepted; central revision

The objection is correct. The draft's qualifications about packages and lost justification do not adequately control its categorical claims. Even with the same process mixture under each metaphysics, conditioning on the target report can make an initially neutral control error favor one account. No assumption of infallibility is needed.

Section 3 will replace the headline neutrality argument with a model of **joint evidence**. Its background will include the agreed semantics but exclude the target report J and the control error E. It will distinguish P(E|F,K) from P(E|F,J,K), and compare P(J,E|F,K) with its modal counterpart. A new numerical illustration uses two fallible process states: R with target-report likelihoods .8/.2 and control-error probability .1, and H with target likelihoods .5/.5 and error probability .8. With equal process and theory priors and conditional independence, E has probability .45 under either theory, yet confidence in F falls from .65 after J to approximately .5333 after J and E. The example is a mathematical illustration, not measured psychology.

I retain only the genuinely conditional result: evidence with equal likelihood **against the same relevant background** supplies no relative likelihood advantage. Sharing a verbal mechanism description does not establish equality. The target report is explicitly evidence available to an observing researcher, not a subject's belief automatically counted as additional evidence for itself.

### M2: Williamson's actual transfer bridge — accepted; qualified assessment rather than rebuttal

The published argument offers an analogy and a mechanism, not generic fallibility. Section 4 will include the predecessor/successor pattern and the inverse-singleton control. These strengthen the case for transfer because they match the direction and phenomenology of the target judgments, including the same singleton. Williamson's §5 already supplies the transfer question and methodological recommendations; I will credit that priority.

The remaining issue is comparative diagnosis. Relational misreading can also produce negative predecessor and positive successor judgments under an iterative conception, and can reproduce the inverse-singleton error. That diagnosis ceases to generate an error when a membership relation is actually asserted. Thus the observed pattern supports caution, but does not uniquely establish the broader heuristic. I do not infer equal comparative support from compatibility. Psychological weights and the strength of the abductive analogy remain unresolved. A demonstrated stalemate or rebuttal of Williamson would exceed the available evidence.

The constant-function control is retained as a transparent illustration of the logical boundary, not offered as a substitute for Williamson's richer examples.

### M3: Fine's limited variables claim and the residual mechanism — accepted

Fine expressly allows other errors and targets a purely linguistic filter. The revision will not attribute universal immunity to him. Section 5 will credit a genuine evidential success against a filter tied to the displayed constituent, conditional on that filter's specification and on no relevant cue being supplied elsewhere.

The post-reference proposal is different: someone understands membership, then substitutes ordinary explanatory usefulness for constitutive truth. Reference tracking does not entail this substitution or even a descriptive paraphrase. The proposal establishes a possibility, not an actual cause, an equally supported competitor, or a transferred diagnosis inheriting the controls' credentials. Its plausibility is limited by the general explanatory-helpfulness account Williamson himself offers; the cognitive-content distinction also has an explicit predecessor in Williamson 2022. Section 6 will constrain one audience-sensitive version and state what would count against it. That restricted test is not a refutation of every broader heuristic.

### m1: Structured contents versus modal essentiality — accepted

Section 2 will separate the identity of semantic content from the determination of essentiality. A modalist can retain distinct structured contents while assigning their essentiality by modal profiles. Distinct contents do not establish distinct essentiality. The reference stipulation also does not settle Fine's §2 dispute about ordinary functional expressions in essentialist contexts.

### m2: Nondiscriminating experimental suggestions — accepted; replaced

Complexity and fresh/familiar-label suggestions are too loosely specified. Section 6 will instead contrast a restricted process that uses helpfulness to a currently primed ordinary audience with a restricted, mastered-content constitutive process. It specifies a fixed proposition, a change in audience-helpfulness ratings, checks of reference and the unchanged metaphysical question, predicted judgment patterns, and a result against the audience-sensitive hypothesis. Effects from newly noticed reasons or misunderstanding would make the comparison inconclusive. No experiment has occurred.

### Uncertain issue: Fine's theoretical support — acknowledged and assessed

Section 7 will discuss his §4 appeal to essentialist logic, semantics, iterative conceptions and well-foundedness. Useful formal articulation is real theoretical work, not just another initial intuition. But soundness and completeness do not by themselves establish that the intended worldly interpretation is true. Explaining well-foundedness through definitions also requires an independently credible link between real definition and construction. Whether these commitments have enough independent support is not resolved here. The essay will not present theoretical support as wholly absent, or restrict legitimate support to laboratory findings.

## Review B

### Attribution and locators — accepted

The original three entries were real and the central paraphrases were supported. The revision will retain them and add pinpoint section references at each load-bearing discussion. Fine 1994 will be cited as a selectively consulted author manuscript, revised February 1993; its source-rendering lines 275–333 are the inspected original passage, not independently established final-journal pagination. Published §3 and §4 references will locate the detour, transfer, variables and theoretical-support arguments. Captured parser line labels belong in the source register, not as invented permanent paragraph numbering.

### Closest literature and contribution boundary — accepted

The general bridge requirement, reference/aboutness distinction, content/guise distinction and comparison-sensitive debunking are not claimed as discoveries. The revision's contribution is a local reconstruction of how the two controls interact once graded reliability and the target report are included. It asks what changes in support each control permits, rather than announcing that neither provides evidence.

I have consulted the assigned primary passages of Williamson 2022, White 2010 and Berker 2009, and additionally retrieved White's relevant original passage. All three will be acknowledged and included in the bibliography with version/read limits. White's caution motivates the observer/report formulation; Berker supplies a related, but morally situated, precedent for separating process identification, relevance and asymmetric comparison. None is credited with the exact paired analysis unless its read passage supports that attribution.

The 2024 and 2026 Williamson chapter bodies and Vavova's article remain access-limited leads in the inherited review. I have not read their bodies and will not cite them for substantive claims. Their failures and unresolved priority questions will be preserved in provenance. No exhaustive novelty finding follows from the finite assigned search or this revision.

### Crucial inference A: joint evidence and graded reliability — accepted

This is addressed by the central change responding to A–M1. Both reviews' distinct countermodels were considered; neither is empirical evidence. The new calculation will be checked independently and retained under sources. The observer's report model does not claim a general reduction of first-person philosophical justification to likelihoods of one's own assent. A substantive argument may survive a defeated report-based route.

### Crucial inference B: possibility, prediction and comparative support — accepted

Two intelligible explanations need not predict an observation equally well. The manuscript will remove the categorical assertion that persistence therefore fails to choose between them. Persistence can favor a content-sensitive account over a strictly surface-triggered account. Whether it favors genuine constitutive insight over a post-reference shortcut requires additional assumptions. The shortcut's existence and comparative credibility are explicitly unresolved.

1. Narrow the contribution and credit predecessors: §§1 and 7.
2. Add local references and semantic-dispute boundary: §§1, 2, 4, 5 and 7; bibliography.

5. Separate accommodation, differential prediction and warranted comparison: §§3–6.
6. Constrain one residual mechanism and reduce ambition: §6; no broad program of metaphysical cognition is promised.

## What is retained, and what remains unresolved

I retain the conditional control, the distinction between term substitution and modal reduction, and the warning against automatic victory. I do **not** retain an application claiming actual equal evidential support, an established psychological diagnosis, or that either philosopher's complete position has been refuted.

The substantive limits remain visible in the manuscript: actual mechanism prevalence and transfer, model priors, the epistemic status of the ontological-dependence premise, the independent force of theoretical support, and the exact priority of the paired formulation. These limits prevent a metaphysical verdict and an exhaustive contribution claim. The revised manuscript is a conditional local argument, not a resolution of essence versus necessity.
