## 2. Closest additional literature

**Williamson 2022, “Metametaphysics and semantics”: close conceptual predecessor.** In §4, author-draft pp. 19–23, especially HTML/PDF text L439–488 and L533–544, semantic value is separated from cognitive guise; guises can be finer than expression types and need not be linguistic. Thus the general distinction between displayed notation and the representations used in thought is already available, including in Williamson's own work. This does not establish the particular post-reference heuristic proposed here, but limits its conceptual novelty. Read the author draft, not a fully compared final edition. [Primary manuscript](https://www.philosophy.ox.ac.uk/files/metaphilosophy2021pdf); `web_B08.json`, `web_B12.json`; publication metadata in `web_B14.json`.

**White 2010, “You just believe that because…”: close epistemological predecessor and counterpressure.** §4.1, author-manuscript pp. 9–11, distinguishes defeating a particular supporting argument from defeating every justification for a belief, and examines screening off evidence by an alternative explanation. Crucially, White cautions against treating one's own belief as though its occurrence were normally one's evidence. The draft must say whether its observations are a subject's intuitions, another researcher's reports about those intuitions, or higher-order evidence about reliability. These are not automatically interchangeable. White does not discuss this singleton/variables pair. [Primary manuscript](https://web.mit.edu/rog/www/papers/youjust.pdf), L434–495 in `web_B11.json`; [published identity](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1520-8583.2010.00204.x), `web_B14.json`.

**Berker 2009, “The Normative Insignificance of Neuroscience”: related but distinct comparative argument.** Section V, published pp. 321–326, especially pp. 324–325, distinguishes identifying a process, evaluating the relevance of its inputs, and establishing an asymmetric advantage over rival intuitions. This is an actual predecessor of the demand not to award one theory a victory from a criticism that fails to distinguish its rival. The context is moral psychology, not functional-term semantics or essence; it does not establish prior possession of the essay's complete paired argument. [Published paper on the author's university site](https://sberker.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum4776/files/sberker/files/berker-norm-insignif-neuro.pdf), PDF indices 28–33, `web_B08.json`, `web_B11.json`.

## 3. What the crucial inference establishes

### A. Conditional neutrality is not yet neutrality of debunking

At draft L49–57, the equal-likelihood equation is correct **if** A fixes the same error distribution under both theories. A's short verbal description alone does not fix that distribution; equality is an additional modeling restriction. The resulting conditional lesson is useful but modest.

The central comparison should distinguish:

- **E:** a demonstrable error in the detour controls;
- **J:** the apparently anti-modal singleton judgment;
- **A:** the process connecting those observations.

Learning E can weaken J's support for F without E directly favoring M over F in isolation. This need not depend on anyone having assumed infallibility. A fallible but mostly trustworthy judgment process can lose credibility to a less trustworthy process.

Here is a reviewer-constructed countermodel, not an empirical estimate or borrowed source example. Give two processes H and R equal prior probability under both metaphysics. Let E have probability .9 under H and .1 under R, independent of the metaphysics conditional on the process. Let J have probability .9 under F with either process; under M let its probability be .9 with H but .1 with R. Assume E and J are conditionally independent given metaphysics and process. E is equally likely under F and M, and the draft's conditional equalities hold. Nevertheless, J's likelihood ratio for F over M drops from .9/.5 = 1.8 before E to .9/.82, approximately 1.10, after E. The control error undercuts a route to F without directly refuting F.

The draft recognizes asymmetric loss at L83–89; that is a genuine strength. But it illustrates the issue with a defeated no-confusion assumption and then moves to repaired packages. It should expressly cover graded reliability and the joint evidence E-and-J. Otherwise the headline equal-likelihood result may look like a response to a stronger debunking inference than it actually addresses. This is a limitation of the proposed contribution, not an established contradiction within the draft.

### B. Variables: a plausible residual mechanism, not demonstrated equal evidential weight

At L61–77, a sound judgment and a post-reference explanatory shortcut can both produce the surviving verdict. That establishes non-entailment: persistence does not guarantee truth-tracking. It does not establish that persistence supplies **no comparative evidence** unless the accounts give it the same likelihood, with comparable independent support. Mere ability to accommodate an observation is weaker than predicting it.

The potentially distinctive move is the account of retrieving an associated description after assigning a simple label. But reference tracking does not itself entail descriptive paraphrase, much less an illicit transition from explanatory unhelpfulness to falsity. At L75 the draft correctly makes a conditional proposal. Keep that status visible elsewhere. Do not replace an overly literal word filter with an unconstrained hypothesis that survives every conceivable intervention.

The suggestions at L107–109 could become a contribution if they specify competing predictions. As written, complexity, familiarity and salience might affect attention or comprehension under either account. Specify one contrast for which the proposed mechanisms predict different response patterns, what comprehension check is required, and what result would count against the residual heuristic. No experiment has been performed and none should be implied.

## 4. Required changes and remaining contribution

1. **Narrow the contribution statement at L17.** Credit the already published transfer diagnosis, the distinction between semantics and cognitive access, and the general comparative-debunking problem. Present the paired application as the proposed advance.
2. **Add precise locators** at the detour discussion, the variables discussion, and the treatment of theoretical support. Acknowledge that the stipulated semantics grants one side of a live semantic dispute for a limited purpose.
3. **Make the evidential object explicit.** Separate control error, target judgment, and joint higher-order evidence. Explain how the equation bears on an actual route of justification rather than only on fixed packages.
4. **Engage the strongest existing replies.** Address the actual process-transfer analogy, not bare fallibility; address the limited variables control, not a claim to universal immunity. These points can be made without abandoning the essay's neutrality.
5. **Distinguish possibility, prediction and comparative support.** This is especially important at L67 and in the concluding diagnostic rule. Equal fit is a modeling result requiring assumptions, not a consequence of two stories being intelligible.
6. **Either constrain the residual mechanism or reduce its ambition.** One discriminating prediction would be more valuable than another general warning about overfitting. Keep a short paper-sized scope rather than promising a science of metaphysical cognition.

**What remains:** an accessible, potentially useful local synthesis: two interventions can remove different defects without identifying the source of a disputed judgment, and a notation-level control may leave a cognition-level process untouched. The detour construction makes this vivid; the paired framing organizes the disagreement. What does not remain as a defensible claim is a newly discovered reference/aboutness distinction, a new equal-likelihood principle, a demonstrated psychological explanation, or a resolution of essence versus modality.

**Unresolved:** whether earlier debunking, experimental-philosophy or metaphilosophy work already states this exact pair; whether the unread 2024/2026 chapter bodies contain an equivalent formulation; and whether either psychological model has independent empirical support.
