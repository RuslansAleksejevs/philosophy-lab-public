A confidential service may reasonably avoid recording its sessions, then receive a complaint it cannot settle from records. The essay argues that it still owes users a workable way to contest its performance and a defensible rule for unresolved claims. Privacy can limit the evidence available without settling who should bear the remaining uncertainty.

## What changed

The revision replaces a broad guarantee with a duty to provide a fair complaint process and justify how unresolved cases are handled. Such a process may end without compensation: a procedural failure alone does not establish a right to repayment. Whether the proposed positive duty is adequately defended remains the central question.
