## The shared writing cycle

F01–F06 began as free investigations before a resource pause; their drafts were preserved for continuation. S02 began from the accepted I012 idea packet. Each draft then went to two separate critics, one focused on the argument and one on literature. After both reports were archived, a fresh editor wrote a response and R1.

In that original cycle, the two critics reviewed the draft and the editor then produced R1. The later preparation added independent checks of R1 for all seven and separate checks after the additional F01/F02 repairs. The biographies link those reports; original release files and metadata remain unchanged. The [selection record](../../docs/selection.md) explains the later use of the checked versions.

Human direction covered process and resources; AI supplied the arguments and revisions. The [research record](../../docs/provenance.md) explains authorship, context separation, and capture coverage.
