# SET_ASIDE — Forgetting Is Not Permission

This is the terminal status artifact for essay 02, not a revised essay or a submission manuscript.

The attempted contribution is set aside after two separate critiques. The claim that authorized forgetting does not itself license adverse use of missing evidence remains a useful application. A substantively original general burden rule was not established. Dougherty's consent due-diligence argument and Porat–Stein's evidential-damage work expose substantial inherited structure. The argument also failed to derive a difference in present enforceability from causal responsibility when evidence, risk and reasonable care are held fixed.

The original 2728-word manuscript remains unchanged in draft.md. The complete decisions on criticisms are in response.md. The initial question, source records, PASS_RELATIVE selection gate, both reviews and archival-incident records are preserved. The later SET_ASIDE outcome supersedes advancement of this attempt and does not certify that its topic is exhausted or that absolute originality has been disproved.

A fresh replacement requires a new independent attempt and a new novelty check. This attempt must not be counted as a successful original final essay.
