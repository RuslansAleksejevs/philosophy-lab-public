## Findings and decisions before manuscript revision

R1 is accepted. Holding confidence fixed while changing tasks isolates a practical causal dependency. The reference-work case does not discover a distinct new norm of belief, and changing the lost task to server maintenance leaves that elementary dependency intact. The controlled-belief/alternative-task test originates in the critic. A defensible repair is to present an applied diagnostic: identify precisely what a confidence-based public appeal is asking people to do, and whether an inquiry objection attacks its recommended contribution or establishes its eligibility for the very truth-producing rationale offered. The result needs no claim of empirical prevalence. It can matter to the classification and burden of an objection even though its rational basis is familiar.

R2 is accepted. James expressly adapts his preaching to different audiences, describes the investigator as interested yet alert to deception, and makes cooperation depend on each person's own duty. Hayward's team framework is already role-sensitive and explicitly requires continued investigation/deliberation. Neither is shown to infer the bad invitation. The opening claim to expose a tension within James and any insinuation of repairing an absent principle in Hayward should be withdrawn. The proposal is a compatible application and explanation of existing resources. These authors' passages remain theirs; their closer selection here was prompted by the critic.

R3 is developed using the critic's test: previous evidence survives cessation of inquiry, so specify a new event to which the old evidence does not transfer. A hypothetical recurring performance has a venue allocated separately each week. A checked allocation last week can warrant that week's appeal that coordinated participation suffices for success. A fresh independent allocation renders last week's result uninformative about this week's venue. Dropping the designated check need not change the allocation or prevent actual success; it prevents using that particular verified-sufficiency rationale unchanged. A merely lower-probability self-fulfilment reason may remain. The example must explicitly distinguish an approaching loss of grounds, loss after the reset, truth of the outcome, and alternative grounds. The venue example and its detailed distinctions are new author development after the critic's suggestion, not pre-critique author content.

R4a is accepted: every inferential step should discuss the same pro tanto reason, never move from one reason to an all-things-considered ought. R4b is accepted: Clifford permits an already convinced person to perform the required inquiry but says conviction impairs its fairness/completeness. No unqualified endorsement by Clifford of the confident checker should be inferred.

## Rejected repair routes

1. Manufacture a contradiction in James or an omission of evidence/roles in Hayward. Rejected because the re-read passages directly undermine that framing.
2. Claim the checker example limits the number of people allowed to believe, while keeping all task assignments fixed. Rejected: everyone can already believe success. The critic's diagnostic defeats this route.
3. Infer that dropping checking instantly destroys all warrant, or that every relevant fact must be rechecked forever. Rejected because evidence can be retained and conditions can remain stable.
4. Claim the new venue example eliminates every self-fulfilment reason. Rejected: even with uncertain venue availability, confidence can still raise the chance of success. The particular verified-sufficiency argument loses its basis, not necessarily every reason for optimism.
5. Remove the warrant-only distinction altogether. Available but not chosen: a clearly bounded example can separate the truth of an outcome from entitlement to an ongoing public rationale and make the diagnostic more informative.
